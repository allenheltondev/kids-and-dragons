# Kids & Dragons logo

The approved identity uses a friendly purple dragon head with gold horns, a gold eye, and a gold throat. Pair the symbol with the live-text “Kids & Dragons” wordmark, as on the home screen. Use the symbol alone for small icons.

## Assets

- `dragon-mark.png`: full-resolution transparent raster master, 1254 × 1254.
- `dragon-{16,32,48}.png`: favicon sizes.
- `favicon.ico`: 16, 32, and 48 pixel layers.
- `dragon-180.png`: Apple touch icon size.
- `dragon-{192,512}.png`: larger app/logo exports, transparent and not maskable.
- `preview.html`: live-text wordmark, light/dark surfaces, and actual-size icons.

Production copies live in `packages/client/public`: `brand/dragon-mark.png` uses the 512px export; `favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png`, and `apple-touch-icon.png` are linked from the HTML entry point. Keep these copies in sync when changing the approved master.

Leave clear space around the symbol and avoid cropping the horns or snout. The palette combines violet, warm gold, and deep plum outlines; the background is `#0d0b1a`. The artwork is raster and contains tonal variation.

## Source

Created with the built-in image generation tool and approved by the project owner. Exports use Lanczos resampling. No later design variants are included.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one production logo symbol for Kids & Dragons, a children's fantasy adventure app. A concise friendly dragon head in profile, facing right, with a rounded snout, one large warm golden eye, two small rounded horns and a short curved neck whose silhouette gently suggests a lowercase d. Flat vector-like graphic, bold simple silhouette, deep violet #6550b8 and warm pale gold #ffe8a3, dark plum #21183e outline. Charming and adventurous, not babyish. Extremely economical geometry, thick shapes, no scales, no texture, no gradients, no shadows, no fine details, no lettering, no dice, no shield, no border or enclosing badge. Centered single symbol filling 85 percent of square canvas with safe margins. Truly transparent background. Must remain recognizable as a dragon at 16 and 32 pixels. Deliver just the isolated finished logo symbol, no mockups or presentation sheet.
