## Description

_This is handwritten lore content that the generator must never overwrite._

The Bigfoot is an ancient guardian of the Enchanted Woods, said to have existed since the
first trees took root in magical soil. Travelers who enter the woods with peaceful intent
often catch glimpses of its massive silhouette moving between the elder trees.

### Origins

Legend tells that Bigfoot was once a simple woodland creature that absorbed centuries of
ambient magic, growing to enormous size and gaining a deep connection to the forest itself.
The druids of old considered it a sacred being and left offerings at the bases of the
oldest oaks.

## Combat Notes

Despite its gentle nature, a provoked Bigfoot is a fearsome opponent. Its massive arms
can uproot entire trees, and the moss on its body hardens into natural armor when
threatened. Adventurers are advised to never attack one near its grove.
