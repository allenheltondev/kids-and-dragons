# Burrowfolk figure generation

All figures were generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the approved Burrowfolk concept and existing entity art as references, then fitted to the 1024×1024 RGBA cutout contract with feet at y=900.

- `assets/entities/burrowfolk/assembled.png`: Generic adult Burrowfolk field traveler, broad badger muzzle and face bands, russet fur, mustard coat, clay-red scarf, notebook and field satchel.
- `assets/cast/ma_ruddle/assembled.png`: Older tired and blunt Burrowfolk woman in a worn mustard prospector coat and clay-red scarf, carrying a blank auction notice.
- `assets/cast/tib/assembled.png`: Burrowfolk child in a vivid orange cap and simple travel clothes.
- `assets/cast/dunmore/assembled.png`: Embarrassed, kind Burrowfolk prospector with a modest pick held safely at his side.
