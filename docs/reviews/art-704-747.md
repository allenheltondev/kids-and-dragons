# Songbird and the mini-game art finish

Delivery for #747 and all three phases of #704. The built-in imagegen tool made
the paintings; prompt records are `docs/nameplate-art-prompts.json` and
`docs/mini-game-art-prompts.json`. The fitted files and untouched C2PA originals
are uploaded to S3 through the project's MCP server. Git keeps their lock entries.

## Paintings and mappings

- Songbird: the existing found-look frame, 512 × 192 transparent WebP, with the
  delivered set's nine-slice insets. It is now listed as delivered, so the existing
  Ashspan Bridge reward draws its painting instead of the plain fallback.
- Canal bridge: broken and repaired variants. The broken bank covers the bridge
  approach, beam check and relay, ferry/carry/shoulders branches and hay relay.
  The mended result and sung repair use the repaired bridge.
- Wolf sneak: sleepy cinder wolves and the sausage wagon, with a calm playable lane.
- Grandmother's doorstep: the round curtained window, doorstep, claim flag and
  ridge tower, shared by the introduction and timing game.
- Pinch price board: a house-sized board and ladder, shared by the introduction
  and cause-and-effect sequence. No readable prices are baked into the painting.
- Returning stone cart: a small cart on a quiet road, shared by the introduction
  and small-hum practice, distinct from the later caravan hairpin.
- Practice Meadow: a picnic clearing, gate, bell, pond and sleepy stump around
  an open center, used by all twelve tutorial interactions. It lives under
  `scenes/first-steps/`; the Gemfall paintings remain under `scenes/gemfall/`.

In total, seven scene paintings cover 28 scene mappings. Four new props supply
an empty terracotta bowl, a prospector's pick, a humming stone crumb and a
green/gold mending hum. The full soup bowl could not represent the empty bowls
the narration describes, and a rope loop could not represent the pick.

## Existing prop review

Reused the mule cart for the Brackens' crossing, a cloth blanket for the loose
awning, the sausage wagon for wolf sneak, a rope post for road markers, bell
ribbon for ducking, knotted cords for the archive's yarn, a hand bell for the
bell code, and the stone board for the Titan's sound sequence. Plain paper
replaces the gray-route and price-clue scroll props: the old `prop_word_scroll`
painting unexpectedly depicts a human holding a scroll.

The two fern tracking covers and the quiet-moss fern still fit their woodland
setting. The fern used as a stone crumb and the fern passed as a hum do not,
and were replaced. Unused egg baskets, jars, cups, crates, boxes, bundles and
chairs were inspected rather than assigned arbitrarily. Several associated
activities now live only in `resumeInteractions`, for finishing old saves, so
they do not require new art for fresh play.

## Render review and layout correction

Reviewed 32 interactions on a 1280 × 720 TV and a 360 × 640 phone, including all
twelve Practice Meadow games. No broken-image fallback was reported. Local
captures and contact sheets are under `staging/render704/` and
`staging/review704-sheet-*.jpg`. Songbird was checked with four long names,
with and without titles, on the TV and phones.

Long repeated cues clipped the TV relay, sequence and memory action areas.
Once these games are active or showing their result, the TV now uses the
board's current-task caption/status instead of repeating the full cue above it.
The introduction remains visible before starting, and phone instructions stay
available. The four-hero long-bridge browser regression checks the complete
relay stage and all hero labels stay inside the TV board. The three reviewed
relay stages also passed measured containment checks after the fix.

This changes presentation only. No interaction rules, branches, rewards or
failure consequences were changed. The art URLs require deliberate Gemfall
file-hash updates. The pre-epic continuation now asserts the newly painted
bridge arrival separately; its gameplay digest remains the original one.
Legacy-roll comparisons similarly ignore only the scene's optional art field.
