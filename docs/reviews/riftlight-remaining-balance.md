# Riftlight: remaining chapter review

This completes the authoring review begun in [the first balance revision](riftlight-balance-revision.md). Riftlight has **24 chapters**, not 23. The first pass changed twelve and only screened the rest; this pass reviews and changes all twelve remaining opening/travel chapters. The earlier claim of eleven remaining chapters was a counting error.

## Review approach

Read the goals, openings, method choices, result branches, important merges and efficient story spines. Check alternate equipment/class approaches, losses and recovery against the existing real-engine route matrices. Judge the experience by story movements and consequences; a low minigame count does not exempt a chapter from a pacing review.

All twelve had some routine roll repetition. The correction varies with the chapter: the quay has many actual hazards and needs one routine crowd check removed, while packing, surveying and teaching sequences need broader changes. No extra games or fights are added to fill a checklist. Existing game controls and accessibility remain.

## Material behavior changes

Routine checks no longer produce minor random injuries, torn maps, or retries followed by the same result. Retired failure-only result scenes and their continuity entries are removed. This is an intentional graph change, not just wording. Risks with actual rescue, resource or route consequences stay; awards, purchases and ordinary failure branches outside the retired checks remain.

Preparing the shelf rope now reliably sets `line_ready`. Earning `knob_hooked` by completing or assisting the toss now gives a deterministic haul ashore; missing the toss does not unlock that option. The other river exits remain reachable. The pass's three local notice flags are consumed in its ending; these local variants do not promise new cross-chapter persistence.

The runner's full account versus short urgent report and the ferry's hand versus crackers are ordinary expressive choices with different reactions. They are not presented as major mechanically divergent branches. The pass notice, prepared crossing and river exits provide stronger consequential decisions.
