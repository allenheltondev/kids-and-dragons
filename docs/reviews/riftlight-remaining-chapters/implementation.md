retained as the basis for the design decisions, not as a list of unresolved
faults in today's main branch.

across eleven other chapters, the updated chapter-cohesion skill, and the first
full regional model: **Stone Counters**. Both model chapters target about thirty
minutes of worthwhile play. Family observation is still required to validate
that budget; this PR does not claim measured enjoyment or timing.

## Follow-on story pass

Revised fifty narrated beats across the ten other long regional, strand-ending,
Convergence and finale chapters. This pass brings concrete requests and observable
mysteries forward, replaces repeated procedural framing with character action,
and adds occasional physical humor. It preserves every choice, interaction,
check, gate, flag, reward, scene edge and duration target from the first batch.
It does not complete the larger mechanical redesigns still listed above.

| Chapter | Story emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Whale Road | Ask for help immediately; put the circling whales and the cost of the shortcut in view. |
| Wrong Valley | Follow the map/river disagreement through the return of the water and the handoff to memory ice. |
| Reading Room | Lead with a moving remembered river; make the cards and unanswered questions concrete. |
| Island Due Back | Use the welcome song, the stone and the island's movement as the spine of the visit. |
| Stuttering Flats | Build from a blinking view to agreed signals and the goat's actual crossing. |
| Bone Yard | Let returning objects and crawler reactions demonstrate the place's rule. |
| Wyrm Nest | Keep the creature's comfort and the readers' needs present from approach to outcome. |
| Squall Temple | Show the people, obstructed doorway, island approach and aftermath in distinct movements. |
| Convergence | Make the summoning complication and cord/chime experiment easy to picture. |
| Finale | Keep today's homes at stake, preserve tension at the last seam, and give Corvane room for a humane aftermath. |

The fifty changed passages total 2,967 words versus 2,649 before. No activities
were removed or added to fill time. The original chapter budgets remain planning
targets. Family read-aloud testing should check whether the new cues help children
explain their job and the choices without another adult explanation. Humor should
get out of the way of the summoning decision and final confrontation.

Story-pass verification: 391 focused tests across 31 files pass after updating
the literal introduction citations. Content validation passes 117 checks with
the same two existing Corvane reference warnings. Continuity reports zero new
findings and zero stale allow-list entries. A structural comparison confirms
that the fifty scene edits change narration only.

## Follow-on choice and activity redesign

Whale Road and Wrong Valley are now rebuilt as the next regional pair, following
the story-only pass. See [regional-playtest.md](../../campaigns/continental-rift/regional-playtest.md)
for the decision/payoff mapping, planned thirty-minute budgets and family test.
Whale Road has 38 authored scenes across alternatives; Wrong Valley has 46.
These file totals do not describe a single playthrough or establish its duration.

Whale Road makes shelter/view placement change the handover task, and greeting
practice unlock a different playable final invitation. The whale response and
turn are now activities rather than only narration. The existing early-opening
cost and island report delivery remain intact.

Wrong Valley makes tool/people preparation determine the later recovery task.
Missed tool recovery adds wet-kit care and preserves its cost in the ending.
Crossing misses require a different playable route, with the actual result
carried to the reading room. Incomplete river observations cannot unlock the
full reconstruction. Routine explanation no longer requires a generic roll.

Reading Room, Island, the remaining strand endings, Convergence and the finale
still need their deeper gameplay passes. The fifty-beat prose pass above remains
historical context; the two new designs supersede its whale/valley scene wording.

Regional redesign verification: all 491 focused tests across 32 files pass.
Typechecking passes. Content/graph-proof validation passes 117 checks with the
same two existing warnings; continuity remains clean. The new class-action tests
show the same near-edge input miss normally and succeed with the widened target.
