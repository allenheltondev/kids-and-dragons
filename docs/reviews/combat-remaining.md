# Remaining ordinary encounter pass

Four encounters now ask different tactical questions while retaining their original rewards,
story destinations and nonlethal recovery routes.

| Encounter | Read and act | Consequence and recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ashspan Bridge | Leave the big mimic's committed plank slap; bring every standing friend to the painted landing ramp. | Each crossing is saved. Six rounds protect Tilly's bag; delay reaches the original rescue. |
| Cairnstep Pass | Keep a hero on the painted winter stores and wolves off it for two round ends. | Leaving preserves earned progress but costs time. Five rounds lead to the original lost-hay branch. |
| Headwaters Washout | Roll the ford stone with two nearby turn ends, avoiding a fixed tail sweep and the marked current. | The current pushes a poor stop west without erasing work. Six rounds lead to the original downstream rescue. |
| Bramblewood | Tag three wisps; leave their marked sparks or daze one. Choose whether a thorn shortcut is worth losing two steps next turn. | Slowing leaves steps to escape. Twelve rounds allow a first-level melee hero to finish; delay retains the original bootlace/snack setback. |

The three Riftlight boards reuse painted props and canonical creature art. Bramblewood retains
its original board and creature statistics. Escape and timeout receipts now select their
authored success or rescue paintings, including after reload. Compact work-objective copy
keeps the immediate decision visible at the largest TV text size.
