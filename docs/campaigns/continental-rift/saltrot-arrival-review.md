# Mud Where Ice Should Be

The Restless Coast's arrival (`riftlight-saltrot-arrival`), revised in November 2026 after the
pilot ([bone-yard-review.md](bone-yard-review.md)) against [arc.md](arc.md#restless-coast-teaches-unstable).
It kept its dice, its glassback crab fight, the Attune lesson (gated on `attune_taught`), its
three arrival variants and the woken-glacier-fold line, and lost an unnamed guide, a
task-and-rule goal, failures that changed nothing and an echo vote at the end.

**Beat.** Puddle's town door is open, and the party knows the silt that buried it came from the
glacier. **Question** (scene 1): *Why is glacier silt on a mudflat?* **Adds:** the cold gray silt
(a p2), Ossley's "came down the river with the melt", and the Listening Chime leaning north toward
the glacier when the flats hum (a p2). The close sees the buried bridge, with snow falling only on
it, and Seren names Orin, who is waiting there.

**Who carries it.** Puddle, the Skupper guide, introduced here for the whole Coast: she guides
from pools, signals with her crest, puffs up (and stings), keeps a floating lunch box, and fits
every puddle. She wants her door open and her paths trusted, so Grandmother Winkle (strict, kind
underneath) will believe her. Dab, Fen and Old Ossley each want something from the party. Pip
arrives with Seren, introduced in words true whether or not a party met him in the Sky; his
"knock loud" spoon is Lady Olivia's way.

## Rhythm

| Movement | What the table does |
| --- | --- |
| The door (≈8 min) | Cross the mud (rolled); climb the shells (sequence game); dig the door (rolled; a thornguard braces); the town's welcome |
| The flats (≈9 min) | The cold silt; Fen's bag or new markers (rolled); Dab puffed and drifting (rolled, or a songkeeper's song); Ossley aground (rolled) |
| Listening (≈6 min) | Rest with Grandmother (her story, or the honeycake gift); Seren's lesson and the hum (rolled, four ways) |
| The tide (≈8 min) | Follow the stripe (rolled; Fen's charm or a flier); the crabs' warning (rolled); **hold the bank or lead the crabs to warm mud** (fight or roll); the early tide (rolled) and the last channel (timing game); the bridge; the campaign question with this piece |

## Consequences

| Choice or roll | Later |
| --- | --- |
| A slow shell climb | No time for the drain trench at the door |
| A half-dug door | Grandmother's line at camp and the ending, unless the honeycake gift wins her blessing |
| Fen helped or soaked | The river charm, and its way along the stripe |
| Dab towed or not | The honeycake (and so the gift) |
| Ossley helped or late | Five coins or three |
| A failed listen: Pip's BONG | The crabs wake too cross to be led; only the fight remains |
| Hold the bank (force) / warm mud (understand) | Won: a luckstone, the quick run at the tide, and cold crabs looking for a door; lost: the families' rescue, and the crabs keep the warm bank and the quick way; led: crabs settled for good, but the tide is at your knees, so no run |
