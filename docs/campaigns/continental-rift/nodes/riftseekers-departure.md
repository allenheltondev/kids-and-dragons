# Node design — The Riftseekers (`riftseekers-departure`)

**Status:** revised in November 2026 against [arc.md](../arc.md) (Opening). Depth: revise. **This chapter carries the campaign:** every later chapter calls back to it.

**Question:** *What are these travelers looking for, and where do we start?* (scene 1, in Orin's words). **Beat:** the party has met the Riftseekers, holds the Listening Chime, and has a question to chase.

**What every route carries out** (each on every path; the tests prove it):
1. **Orin, Lady Olivia and Seren Wisp** by name in scene 1, with wants: Orin to notice what does not match, Olivia to use the openings, Seren to listen.
2. **Orin's map does not match** (`map_spread` → `map_spot` → `map_wrong`, the p1): the herder's path is water, cairns climb where the map shows a cliff (the Cairnstep, which the Frostfang griffins say was not there last season). Orin says the campaign question aloud, "Something is moving the land. What is it? And can we put things right?", and asks the party to remember anything else that does not match.
3. **Olivia's "Imagine if we could use them!"** (`olivia`), and she is the voice of forcing twice, so children can form an opinion of her.
4. **The Listening Chime**, its rules, and its **first lean toward the glacier** (`chime_lean`, p2), witnessed and unexplained.
5. **The three exits**: the shelf toward the Cairnstep, the hidden openings in the glacier, the long bank road to Ashspan Bridge. The close (`set_out`) asks the campaign question again.

Seren still reacts to `starweaver_read_first_rift` (`seren`). The chapter sets no journey flag.

**Consequences (force or understand, twice).**
- *Rush the hay over the soft ice now* (Olivia; quick TN 12, everyone) or *mark a safe bank first* (Orin; clever TN 8). Rushing feeds the herd today: the herder's thanks (a river charm) and a full, happy herd at the send-off; a fall costs warmth. Marking well opens a safe way to the lookout that shows the thin ice; a wobbly line is rubbed out, and the goats go hungry till dark either way.
- *Ring the chime hard* (Olivia) or *hold it still and listen* (Seren; clever TN 8). Ringing stops the hall's song and drops the roof's snow on everyone (1 damage), and Seren and Orin both say "listen first" later. A failed listen reroutes: Olivia grabs the chime and rings it herself.

**Rhythm.** Meeting and the sled (a vote, a relay or a check), the map (two checks and the p1), Olivia's choice, the chime and its lean, Seren's ring trial (kept checks), the roads and the lookout, the wind (everyone rolls), tea (the rest), the last coins, the road choice, the parting and the send-off, the camp (rest) and the close. Cut: three inert games (ring case, echo pattern, send-off) and two echo votes (sketch, map); the old "help them pack" goal is now a question.
