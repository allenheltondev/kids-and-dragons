# The Maps Come Together

Each strand end has two convergence edges, each requiring its own completed key
and one of the other keys. A third strand is optional. Guards use completed
nodes, not loose clues or inventory. The party returns to a sheltered glacier
ledge by the travelers' ordinary paths, carrying accounts rather than relics.

The opening introduces Bram with his job and identifies the returning cast.
Comparisons preserve gaps and incomplete tests; the matching shapes suggest a
pattern without naming the cause. The emperor does not speak or appear here.
Olivia makes one summoning, discovers that its behavior surprises her, and stops
feeding it. The party then helps Bram stop the experiment, stays beside Olivia
while she observes from the boundary, or uses the chime readings shared with
Orin to talk her back. Players never summon an opening themselves.

Pip stays back if the party helps Bram, including the Orin intervention; otherwise
he follows Olivia to the marked working ground. Trust from the whale opening plus
stopping her yields redeemed; trust plus standing with her yields ally; no trust
yields rival in either case. Completion rules set exactly one allegiance for the
next chapter. Olivia is neither punished nor made an enemy for leaving.

Seren's hands-on lesson teaches Manipulate: hold a readiness condition, never
change destination, use limit or return behavior. Every low-roll path finishes
the lesson and completion raises journey attunement to 2. This chapter pays the
agreed 650 XP without bonus objectives. Full-route XP acceptance is still a
separate production gate, because the optional graph has grown beyond its first
size estimate.

The interactive spine has 45 scenes, 15 decisions and six physical checks,
about 27 uncalibrated minutes. Timed family play remains unmeasured. Tests play
all six trust/intervention combinations, exercise the guarded Orin option and
check every key-node subset against all three strand-end entrances.

callbacks are gated independently on the three learned discoveries. Each map
