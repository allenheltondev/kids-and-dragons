# Reading Room and Island: play the discovery

Follow-on regional revision using [chapter-cohesion](../../../.claude/skills/chapter-cohesion/SKILL.md)
and its [play-design reference](../../../.claude/skills/chapter-cohesion/references/play-design.md).
Both chapters retain an intended thirty-minute sitting. No family observations
have been recorded. Budgets below are plans, not measured timings; the older
pacing estimate does not include authored interaction time.

## Reading Room

The extraordinary event is the moving river preserved in ice. Reading now includes
watching its landmark pattern, following a ridge through cloudy layers, recalling
the rock sequence, or assembling two separately collected accounts. Those tasks
replace generic observation rolls. Assisted results stay credited as helped and
do not unlock an independent citation.

| Movement | Family play | Planned minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter and prepare | Quiet shared approach, inspection-card links, rest, choice of close cradle or dry table | 6 |
| Observe and test | Watch the remembered river, choose observer roles, test the second view | 9 |
| Decide what the evidence supports | Cite an independent reading or ask for a signed co-reading; test the catalog route; handle papers according to preparation | 7 |
| Choose who can use the work | Preserve the open questions; share, publish or keep private; play the newcomer task that choice enables | 8 |

| Choice or result | Immediate difference | Later use or cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close cradle | Support the near reading place | Adds a clear-window timing approach; papers remain near meltwater and need the tray catch |
| Dry farther table | Balance the dry work surface | Adds a direct dry-slip relay instead of the tray rescue; close-window options remain unavailable |
| Separate observers | Collect water and ridge accounts separately | Adds the shared-clue second-view reconstruction |
| Missed or assisted second reading | Record the obscured part as unfinished | Independent citation remains unavailable; a keeper can sign her own co-reading without repairing the party's missing detail |

Starweaver Attune follows the close-view clear interval using a .6 target versus
.3 for the ordinary close-view approach. An identical near-edge input is tested
through the engine: it misses normally and succeeds with the class support.
This helps observation; it does not command the memory to reveal a date or cause.
The routine teach-back roll is removed. No private page is handed over simply
because the party helps a newcomer look.

## The Island Due Back

The welcome is a played song-shape memory rather than selecting how to hear a
song and being told it worked. The visit then plays sighting, signals, landing,
copying, orientation and comparison. The joined-land discovery is still revealed
only after the successful test or the party's different helped follow-up.
Recovery leaves the first unfinished test and worn middle visibly unfinished.

| Movement | Family play | Planned minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome and choose a priority | Song shapes, moving sighting, recover letters or steady the station, rest | 7 |
| Reach the island | Repeated signal or continuous watch, checked bearings, landing, any kit recovery | 8 |
| Read the old stone | Prepare the standing place, copy grooves, orient the pages, compare and interpret | 9 |
| Return and act on the knowledge | Keep or share the sketch, choose warning or inspection message, return the chosen kit and dispatch with available help | 6 |

| Existing or refined choice | Implemented later consequence |

| Prepare the signal station | A working frame adds a cloth dispatch instead of preparing and carrying a hand-relay packet |
| Fail a landing and borrow a pocket kit | Keep the message slot and add a light return; the broad copying-frame approach is unavailable |
| Carry the full kit in batches | Play the batch relay and return-kit relay; retain the broad copying frame, but lose the earlier messenger/station dispatch slot |
| Bring the broad copying frame | A .6 copying target instead of .3 changes an actual near-edge outcome; clear copying helps the later comparison |
| Copy or compare with assistance | Preserve the missing detail and helped attribution; do not silently set independent-match flags |
| Send a course warning or invite another reader | Dispatch only the chosen message and retain the temple's existing differing preparation outcome |
| Deliver the promised whale report | The existing one-time delivery gate is consumed separately from stone-sketch permission |

Thornguard Brace secures the landing foot without the centering activity, leaving
the actual stone investigation to play. The approach still needs checking; a
class does not hold either island still. The complete kit is expedition gear,
not a new inventory reward or a copy taken from the stone.

The fallback hand relay remains available alongside earned help. Choosing to
help with the crew's kit remains available alongside a light return. A delayed
relay does not claim that a warning arrived early. None of the dispatches includes
a private sketch unless the party separately authorized sharing it.

## Verification and family observation

The new actual-control suite plays 144 complete route combinations across one,
two and four seats, independent/failed/assisted evidence, priorities, kits,
publication and privacy. Focused cases cover a posted guide that needs help,
class/equipment inputs with different actual results, and Thornguard's saved job.
Existing tests still exercise the whale opening, island revisit behavior,
Reading Room's optional Coast comparison and all incoming campaign routes.

The exhaustive walker still checks every authored branch, flag, actor seat and
continuity prerequisite. It now normalizes interaction nonces for state comparison
and scales its finite state guard with authored graph size; the richer Island
has more legitimate independent outcomes. This changes only the test helper,
not the runtime or the authored consequences.

Play both as separate family sittings, including a fast class route and a
miss/recovery route. Time each movement. Ask children to explain the cost before
choosing a priority or kit, and record where the adult must explain a clue. Watch
whether the memory and carving feel like discoveries, whether everyone has a
useful turn, and whether the final dispatch feels earned. Adjust depth in the
specific movement if it runs short or long; do not use mandatory retries or
longer explanations to reach thirty minutes.

A final read-aloud pass trims repeated setup and consequence recaps while keeping
every activity, choice, gate and outcome. The timing budget reserves the recovered
reading time for the new controls and discussion, especially on the Island.
