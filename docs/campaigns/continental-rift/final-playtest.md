Convergence and Finale target approximately thirty minutes each. This replaces the
older 35/40-minute estimates by removing repeated setup/report rolls and playing
the important work directly. These are design budgets, not observed family timing.
The pacing model omits interaction time and predicts roughly 12/20 minutes before
that play is counted. Check efficient solo and class-assisted routes with families.

## Family observation

Try solo, two-player and four-player tables with relaxed/assisted play. Before
choosing, ask what the party gives up and which later job changes. Watch for
confusion between changing the screen cord and moving the chime. Let children
explain why the note can change without changing an opening's destination.

In the finale, check whether keeping a rescue station or reserve team feels like
an understandable preparation. Time the confrontation separately from read-aloud.
After a lost encounter, ask which support has been spent, whether the recovery
feels different, and which routes are still shifted. Give the fate conversation
space; humor from the boot, ribbon and welcome-mat goat should not interrupt it.
