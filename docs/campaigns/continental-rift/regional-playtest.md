# Whale Road and Wrong Valley: consequential regional play

Follow-on implementation using [chapter-cohesion](../../../.claude/skills/chapter-cohesion/SKILL.md)
and its [play-design reference](../../../.claude/skills/chapter-cohesion/references/play-design.md).
Both chapters target about thirty minutes. These are planning budgets, not family
observations. No timer, artificial wait, mandatory replay or extra reward fills time.
The older pacing model excludes the authored activity time, so its minute estimate
must not be reported as a measured session.

## Whale Road

The job is to make room for the whales and travelers before choosing how the folds
open. The existing early-opening/scattering consequence stays in the campaign's
rift action. Ending at the offer does not scatter whales or commit the party to
Olivia's proposal. Guiding in the chapter actually plays the invitation before
setting `whales_guided`, preserving the lock against a later early opening.

| Movement | Play and conversation | Planned minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find the approach | Shared landmark layout; track the notched fin through mist; recall the observed response | 7 |
| Learn a greeting and build a waiting place | Choose note or arm greeting; practice it; retrieve the cloth; choose and build shelter or open view | 8 |
| Make room and hand over | Invite the little whale's turn; rest; teach the newcomer through a relay or direct cloth recognition | 8 |
| Decide who benefits | Choose the report commitment and early-opening offer versus guiding; play the chosen invitation if guiding | 7 |

| Informed decision | Immediate work | Later consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet note or arm greeting | Timing or shared support | Unlocks timing the final invitation or holding the visible greeting; keeper-led relay remains available |
| Sheltered wait or exposed view | Support the sheltered cloth or balance its exposed poles | Shelter requires a watcher relay; open view adds direct signal-memory handover; a missed direct handover reroutes through the relay |
| Guide or hear the early-opening offer | Play the invitation, or leave the pair unopened | Guiding keeps whales on their route and prevents the later early-opening choice; the offer preserves the campaign's existing consequential decision |
| Carry a report or leave local copies | Explicit commitment | The existing island-lookout delivery option consumes `sky_followup_promised`; this pass preserves that producer/consumer pair |

A Songkeeper's note-practice target is .6 wide versus .3, implemented by the
existing timing mechanic. It helps with learning, without claiming to command a
whale. A missed greeting leads to a slower keeper-led balance task. A missed whale
invitation leads to a quiet shared contribution while the keeper guides, not a
retry or a penalty to the whales. No new painted whale motion is promised: the
existing controls and authored reactions carry the action.

## The Wrong Valley

The river's unexplained change remains a witnessed mystery. The chapter preserves
the Stone Counters road-plan bonus, the actual high/low route callback in Reading
Room, and the two checked river outlines. It introduces no cause, date or new
campaign-ending fact.

| Movement | Play and conversation | Planned minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the changed bank | Choose a landmark comparison, with an easier carried-road-plan option; test the calm water pocket | 6 |
| Prepare one priority | Hoist and tie the tools high, or rig and test the worker's upper handline; share crossing signals; rest | 8 |
| Cross and watch the change | Play the chosen crossing and any alternate-route recovery; observe the river's return | 8 |
| Finish the unprepared job | Guide the worker or recover the tools; deal with wet gear if needed; reconstruct honest evidence and teach the warning | 8 |

| Informed decision or result | What remains ready | Later task, cost or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tools first | Bundle on a dry high rack | Pass lantern directions to bring the last low watch onto the upper path |
| People first | Worker can use the prepared upper handline herself | Hook the low tool bundle; a miss gets the kit wet and adds a drying-board activity; the ending retains the cleaning cost |
| Let the workers finish | Everyone has a safe way onward | Move replacement cord and drying supplies; unprotected low tools get wet, while previously raised tools stay dry |
| Low/high route misses | The other route remains checked | Play cooperation on the high backup or balance on the low backup; record the route actually used for Reading Room |
| Complete/incomplete return observation | Checked before-and-after landmarks always remain | A complete observation unlocks a reconstruction from the full pattern; otherwise use a shared landmark sequence and leave the hidden detail blank |

Duskrunner placement and recovery targets widen from .3 to .6; a near-edge input
that misses ordinarily succeeds with the class approach. Thornguard Brace skips
the board-support activity while leaving the route signal, crossing, river event
and recovery to play. Neither ability makes the river follow orders.

## Playtest and verification

Run each chapter as a standalone sitting, with one, two and four connected heroes.
Time the four movements separately. Include one efficient success route and one
miss/recovery route; use both central preparation priorities. Have children explain
what the chosen plan leaves for later before confirming it. Do not supply that
answer for them.

Watch for repetitive tapping, clues an adult must translate, a solo hero excluded
from shared work, and whether the final whale turn and returning water feel like
the adventure rather than another setup. Check the efficient class route as well
as the full party route. If a session is short, add worthwhile discovery or a
changed task to that movement; do not pad the reading or require misses.

The new engine-control suite covers 96 full decision/outcome/party combinations,
three Thornguard assisted routes, and an actual near-edge comparison for three
class-assisted actions. It checks changed jobs, available choices, honest endings,
goals, and evidence. Existing exhaustive walks cover other gates and histories.
The content, continuity and graph-proof checks remain required. Automated success
is not a claim about observed family enjoyment or thirty-minute timing.
