# Camp friends and nameplate art

Delivery for #659, #665 and #680. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool;
untouched originals and fitted paintings are stored through the project's MCP
server. Prompts and provenance are in `camp-friend-art-prompts.json` and
`nameplate-art-prompts.json`.

- **#659:** the spotted kid goat now sits with a raised front hoof, a patched
  brown-and-cream coat and a teal collar. The owner chose the coat/collar and
  requested a different pose. Bun stays as painted. Its `FRIEND_PAINT` box is
  remeasured. Two four-player camp captures checked all six current friends;
  the duckling, mosshorn, harbor gull and silver otter need no repaint.
- **#665:** ribbon, leafy and starry are delivered; plain keeps the existing
  unframed name. All use the manifest's 512 × 192 transparent WebP canvas and
  64/48/64/48 nine-slice insets.
- **#680:** the owner approved four earned designs. Golden keeps its existing
  Mythic unlock. The Master design is reused for the four class-specific
  receipts; the campaign design is reused for each of the four campaigns.
  Camp Friends unlocks on the first friend. Repeated designs have identical
  bytes in S3, with distinct catalog paths as the current contract requires.

The frames were inspected on the TV lobby cards, story lineup and phone
surfaces with four long names, with and without titles. Captures are under
`test-results/art-659-665-680/` locally. This exposed dark inherited text in the
phone's gold active-player strip; framed names now keep light ink with a dark
outline. The art gate also checks every delivered frame's size, transparency
and solid text center.
