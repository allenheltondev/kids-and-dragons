{{/* ===== What is it ===== */}}

What is {{ site.Params.brand.name }}?

{{ .Content }}

Tap, don't type

Every choice is a big picture to tap. The only thing you ever type is your hero's name, and "Surprise me" does that too.

TV and phones

The story plays on the big screen; each phone is a controller. On the road, phones alone do both.

Nobody dies

At zero you are knocked down, and a friend helps you up. A lost fight sends the story somewhere new, never to a game-over screen.

A chapter a night

About thirty minutes a sitting. Heroes keep their levels and treasure from one chapter to the next.
