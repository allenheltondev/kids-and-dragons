Welcome to the Realm

The map says it plainly, right on the mountain: "Source of the Gemfalls, and restless power." The Realm of Red Sky is a world where the forest remembers, the dead sleep uneasy at the river mouth, and every road, ship, and rumor eventually converges on a city that belongs to no one. Thirteen regions, one continent-splitting river, and at least one door nobody has ever opened.

This wiki is the realm's canonical design bible. Everything here is connected to everything else — every creature knows who it likes and why, every river failure sweeps you somewhere real, and every page links onward. The fastest way in: pick a pin on the map above, or chart a course below.
