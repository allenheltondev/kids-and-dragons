{{/* Whether a page's subject is in a story yet, from `played_in` (the chapters that use it, written by tools/wiki/chapter-uses.ts). Returns a dict: known true when the generator read the chapters for this page unmet true when no chapter uses it yet (badge "Not in a story yet") titles the chapter titles that use it, in play order Heroes, campaigns and quests never take the badge: the heroes are always the party, and a campaign is a story rather than something in one. */}} {{- $known := isset .Params "played_in" -}} {{- $ids := .Params.played_in | default slice -}} {{- $type := .Params.type | default "" -}} {{- $unmet := and $known (eq (len $ids) 0) (not (in (slice "character" "campaign" "quest") $type)) -}} {{- $titles := slice -}} {{- range $ids -}} {{- with index site.Data.game.chapters . -}}{{- $titles = $titles | append .title -}}{{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return (dict "known" $known "unmet" $unmet "titles" $titles) -}}
