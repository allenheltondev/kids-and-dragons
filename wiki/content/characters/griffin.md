## Lore

> *Half eagle, half lion, entirely certain you should not have climbed that.*

Griffins keep the aeries of the Frostfang and, by long tradition, the realm's northern watch. A griffin's first flight is a rescue drill and its hundredth is usually a rescue, and the two facts are connected in exactly the way griffin parents intend. What the watch sees in the Expanse goes in the watch-log and nowhere else, which is either discipline or the fact that it is almost always nothing. Almost.

### For Fun

- Thermal-riding competitions where form outranks altitude. An elegant spiral beats a high one, and everyone is a judge.
- Flying escort alongside a cloud whale for the length of one song, which can take a day and is worth it.
- Re-stacking watch cairns that did not need re-stacking. It is not fussiness. It is readiness. It is possibly fussiness.

### Getting Along

Formal at first meeting, fierce in friendship, and constitutionally incapable of watching someone struggle up a slope without circling lower just in case. Griffin hospitality is a rescue you did not know you were having.

### Likes

- **[Snowhorn Goat](/creatures/snowhorn_goat/)** — The mountain's own early-warning system. When the goats move, a griffin calls it down the valley, and both halves of the arrangement are proud of it.
- **[Cloud Whale](/creatures/cloud_whale/)** — A storm-song heard early has ended a hundred rescues before they began.
- **[Yeti](/npcs/yeti/)** — Below the ice line, the other half of every rescue.
