## Lore

> *Sworn to watch the sky. Proudest log entry in four centuries: 'nothing.'*

The Skywardens were founded to watch the red sky and answer whatever finally comes out of it, and the order's deepest doctrine is that the watch being boring is the watch working. Recruits arrive dreaming of battle and are handed a ledger; the ones who come to love the ledger stay. Every warden knows the founding oath by heart and no warden knows what, precisely, it was first sworn against, which the order regards not as a gap in the records but as the records doing their job.

### For Fun

- Watch-games: competitive cloud identification, governed by four centuries of accumulated house rules.
- Forge nights at the citadel, where the smithing is real and the storytelling is quality-controlled.
- Sky-cartography — charting a thing that will not hold still, revised nightly, bound annually, shelved forever.

### Getting Along

Formal, reliable, and rigid in exactly the way that makes them excellent in a crisis and exhausting at a festival. A warden's word is a load-bearing structure. The order will spend individuals for the realm and has never pretended otherwise, which is the coldest thing about it and the most honest.

### Likes

- **[Griffin](/characters/griffin/)** — The northern watch shares its logs. Half of warden doctrine is griffin weather.
- **[Centaur](/npcs/centaur/)** — The roads are the realm's nervous system, and the centaurs run it faster than any signal tower.

### Dislikes
