## Lore

As the wardens tell it, the citadel was raised on the highest peak of the foothills for one purpose - to watch the red sky - and every stone of it faces upward. Records of the construction itself are sparse, which the order attributes to the builders having been busy.

### Recorded History

Four centuries of watch rotations, forge-nights, and ledgers - above all the ledgers. The citadel's history is deliberately uneventful, and the order maintains that this is the finest record any fortress has ever compiled.

### Cultural Significance

The foothills' fixed point: caravans navigate by its silhouette, locals set their clocks by the watch-horns, and the whole region sleeps a little easier for the violet windows burning overnight - without, if pressed, being able to say precisely what the wardens are watching for. Neither, precisely, can the wardens.

### Common Beliefs

- As long as the forge smoke rises, the realm is fine. The day it stops, ask questions.
- Somewhere below the citadel is a sealed vault the oaths are about. Wardens neither confirm nor deny, mostly because they were recruits once and would also like to know.

### Disputed Beliefs

- What the founding oath was first sworn against. The order's own records decline to say, and the order regards the declining as the records doing their job.

### Neighbors

- **[Red Sky Foothills](/biomes/red_sky_foothills/)** — Protector and tenant: the citadel watches the sky for the region, and the region keeps the citadel fed, informed, and supplied with recruits who think the job is about swords.
- **[Mount Red Sky](/locations/mount_red_sky/)** — The watch points at the sky, but the windows face the mountain too. The order has never said whether that is architecture or policy.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall has stretched the patrols thin exactly when the foothills got crowded, and the order is discovering that a boom is harder to watch than a mystery.

### Story Hooks

- One year's ledger is bound in a different color and reads, in its entirety, like every other year's. The one question every recruit asks about it receives the traditional answer: 'nothing.'
- An ember storm is due - the old hands can feel it - and the last one is just far enough back that only the ledgers remember what the rite requires.
