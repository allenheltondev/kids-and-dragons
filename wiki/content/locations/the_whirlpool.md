## Lore

Nobody saw it start. The oldest merfolk verses already treat it as old, and the accepted account on both coasts is simply that the sea turns there and always has - the one fixed habit of an ocean that otherwise keeps none.

### Recorded History

Its history is the history of keeping it company: the nightly songs from the cove, sailing customs on two coasts, and generations of harbor-log entries recording that it strengthened with the weather and settled with the tide, exactly as it did the year before.

### Cultural Significance

A landmark visible from two coasts, a hazard with a reputation, and - to the cove - a responsibility. Sailors use it to navigate by staying away from it, which is, as navigation goes, extremely reliable.

### Common Beliefs

- It is counting something. Opinions differ on what, and on what happens when it finishes.
- Drop a small gift in on your first crossing past it. Not because it helps. Because it might, and the gift is small.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether it has a bottom, and whether the merfolk songs restrain it or merely flatter it. The merfolk answer the second question by not missing a night.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- It has widened exactly once: the year the songs went unsung. The merfolk's attendance customs date from that year.

### Neighbors

- **[The Open Sea](/biomes/open_sea/)** — The sea's one permanent address, and its most honest signage: when the Whirlpool runs loud, everything with a keel gives the southeast a wider margin.
- **[Mermaid Cove](/biomes/mermaid_cove/)** — The cove keeps its appointment nightly; the Whirlpool, so far, keeps its diameter.
- **[The Bone Yard](/locations/bone_yard/)** — Visible from the Yard's shore, turning past the realm's quietest residents; the currents between them have opinions about small boats.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall shipping lanes keep shaving the margin to save a day, and the season's tally of 'carried off course, arrived embarrassed' is at a record.

### Story Hooks

- Things surface at the rim occasionally - never wreckage, always small objects, always polished. The otters are suspected. The otters are always suspected.
- A becalmed boat once circled the rim for three days and came ashore at the cove with a crew who all agreed the Whirlpool had been humming, and who all hummed it differently.
