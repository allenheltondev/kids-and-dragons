## Lore

The accepted account is that the founders wanted neighbors, but with notice, and planted accordingly. The hedges came first and the town grew inside them - grown thick, grown tall, and, the founders' one indulgence, grown opinionated.

### Recorded History

Town history is hedge history: plantings, graftings, the season the paths first changed on their own, and the long friendly record of keepers learning that tending the hedges and commanding them are different jobs.

### Cultural Significance

The Sunward Fields' far waystop and its filter: the first roof after the bridge road or the last one before it, depending on your direction, and a town that decides - or whose hedges decide - who gets through.

### Common Beliefs

- The hedge hears offers. Flattery gets you nowhere; sincerity gets you the short path.
- If today's path through the hedges is longer, it is for a reason. If it is shorter, check what you are carrying.
- Never prune anything yourself. That is the keepers' whole job, and the hedge's whole sense of humor.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the hedges decide who gets through or the keepers do. Visitors assume the keepers. The keepers, notably, do not.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The hedges decide. The keepers advocate, negotiate, and occasionally plead, but the hedges genuinely do not listen to anyone who has not been let in properly - including, on bad days, the keepers.

### Neighbors

- **[The Sunward Fields](/biomes/sunward_fields/)** — A Fields town to the bone - cooperative, festival-keeping, fed by the same harvest road it watches over; waystop duty is its contribution to the cooperatives.
- **[Stone Crossing](/locations/stone_crossing/)** — Bridge news arrives with every wagon, and a queue at the crossing is a full inn at Bramblewood.
- **[The Exchange](/locations/exchange/)** — Far ends of the same road: the city sends travelers, prices, and fashion; Bramblewood sends produce, gossip with the mud still on it, and travelers it has already vetted.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall traffic wants through the hedges in a hurry, and the hedges do not do hurry; the inn is full, the paths are long, and the queue outside the north gap has developed its own economy.

### Story Hooks

- Once in the town's history the hedges opened a path straight through - both gaps, one line, no turns. Nobody now living knows for whom, and the keepers' records note only the date and, underneath, 'as agreed.'
- A door-sized gap in the oldest hedge wall has been sealed with thorns as long as anyone remembers. The hedge maintains it - the way you maintain a thing you intend to use again.
