## Lore

The accepted account is that the trolls built the bridge because the river was being unreasonable, and that the settlement is, at bottom, a queue that put down roots.

### Recorded History

Crossing history is toll history: generations of stories, riddles, and promises paid at the bridge, the long riddle account with the eastern prides, and a flood-roll of the years the stones went under - when the ferry becomes, briefly and repeatedly, the realm's most important vessel.

### Cultural Significance

The realm's bottleneck, and proud of it: every eastern story crosses here eventually, pays something that is not money, and leaves a little better told.

### Common Beliefs

- Pay the drake, answer the troll, and tip the ferryman in song. The crossing is cheap if you count right.
- A riddle owed at the bridge is a debt. The interest is embarrassment.
- Never brag about the current. It auditions people.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the troll and the drake operate the crossing in partnership. Travelers assume a cartel; regulars point out that the rates have never once been coordinated.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The troll and the drake have never exchanged a single word. Each privately believes the other founded the arrangement, and is too professional to raise the subject now.

### Neighbors

- **[The Great River](/features/great_river/)** — The river is the reason, the obstacle, and the business model. The crossing exists because the Great River narrows here once, briefly, and has never been talked into doing it anywhere else.
- **[The Sunward Fields](/biomes/sunward_fields/)** — The western approach is Fields country, and a long queue at the bridge is the best market day the nearby cooperatives get.
- **[The Eastern Plains](/biomes/eastern_plains/)** — The eastern side is pride country, where escorts begin and end and the riddle account is honored; the bridge is the seam between two halves of the realm, stitched daily.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall traffic has produced the longest queues in the crossing's history, and the petition for a second bridge has reached the troll, who is weighing the question aloud. Estimates for the answer run from seasons to decades.
- The drake has raised its rates, citing volume. The queue's opinion of this is loud; the drake's opinion of the queue's opinion is underwater.

### Story Hooks

- The bridge's central span contains one stone that matches no local rock, and the troll, asked about it, begins the answer so slowly that no traveler has yet had time to hear the end.
- The sixty-year riddle on the pride account is the troll's to answer, and the troll is still weighing it, one word per season. Both ends of the bridge agree that whoever interrupts will carry the blame forever.
