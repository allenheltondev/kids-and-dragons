## Lore

No accepted account survives contact with the rock itself: sailors say the sea carved a lucky likeness, cave-divers say no current carves eye sockets level, and the stone trolls, who keep the outer galleries, say only that it was already old when their oldest stories were young.

### Recorded History

The cave's recorded history is a list of thresholds: how far the mapped galleries reach, and the dates each expedition pushed the map one chamber deeper or declined to. The deep record is mostly names, dates, and the phrase "turned back," written in every era's handwriting.

### Cultural Significance

The realm's agreed front door to the underground, and the reason coastal charts mark depth in two directions. Sailors give the skull a courteous margin, and the trolls give it maintenance - the only attention it has ever visibly accepted.

### Common Beliefs

- Knock before entering. It is not superstition, it is manners, and something in there keeps track of manners.
- Steady in, steady out. The cave dislikes surprises more than it dislikes visitors.
- The tide decides your visiting hours. Argue with the tide, not with the cave.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the skull resembles any creature that ever lived. Scholars have compared it to every remains in the Bone Yard and matched nothing, which has settled the question for nobody.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- Parts of the cave sound designed because they are. The echo hunter has spent lifetimes adjusting drips and moving pebbles until its chambers ring true - the deep galleries are one creature's centuries-long housekeeping, a fact that would comfort no expedition.

### Neighbors

- **[The Open Sea](/biomes/open_sea/)** — The sea is the doorstep and the doorman: every approach is by water, and the water sets the schedule.
- **[Frostfang Peaks](/biomes/frostfang_peaks/)** — The aeries watch the skull from their coast and give it flight-rule distance; griffin manners, like troll manners, extend to doors.
- **[The Bone Yard](/locations/bone_yard/)** — The realm's two great deposits of enormous old bone, forever compared by scholars and never yet matched; whatever the skull was, the Yard did not bury its relatives.

### Current Tensions

- Unfamiliar lanterns have been seen in the outer galleries at hours no expedition claims, and the trolls have begun greeting visitors with one more question than usual.
- The last two mapping expeditions came back early, in agreement that the deep chambers sounded 'rearranged,' and in disagreement about everything else.

### Story Hooks

- The oldest troll story about the cave calls it a door without saying to where, and troll stories are not known for leaving out the important part by accident.
- A missing expedition's ledger washed out of the cave mouth a year after they went in - dry, complete, and one entry longer than when it went in.
