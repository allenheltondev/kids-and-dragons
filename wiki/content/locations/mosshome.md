## Lore

The accepted account, preserved in the settlement's own archive, is that the first bigfoot families asked the trees, and the asking took forty years. The founding agreement survives in full, and visitors are sometimes disappointed to learn that its clauses are mostly about compost.

### Recorded History

MossHome's history is the archive: path-records, plant journals, weather memory, and the settlement's own growth rings, kept continuously since founding. The famous entry is the Reshelving - every path in the Woods moved in one night, and the archive's marginal note, in an unidentified hand, reads "expected."

### Cultural Significance

The realm's memory of the west. Healers, historians, and the politely desperate come to consult records nobody else thought to keep: which winters the moths came early, what the marsh whispered forty years ago, how long a promise has left to run.

### Common Beliefs

- Do not eat the record. The calendar gardens are the week, the month, and dessert only by archival approval.
- A door grown shut is an answer. You may ask again next season.
- If a mosshorn naps against your work, the work is good. This is peer review, and it is binding.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the settlement chose the site or the site chose the settlement. The archive holds both accounts, in different hands, dated the same day.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- One shelf of the archive is written in cultivated moss - living documents that must be watered to stay legible. One is in a strain nobody now living can read, and it is watered anyway, on schedule, because you do not let a book die for being difficult.

### Neighbors

- **[Enchanted Woods](/biomes/enchanted_woods/)** — Steward and tenant at once: MossHome keeps the forest's records and the forest keeps MossHome, and the founding agreement is honored so thoroughly that neither party has needed to reread it.
- **[Whispering Marsh](/biomes/whispering_marsh/)** — Downhill neighbors and professional colleagues: marsh herbs come up, remedies and flood-warnings go down, and the archivists trade notes with the witches in the margins of each other's books.
- **[The Exchange](/locations/exchange/)** — The city sends its unanswerable questions west by caravan, and MossHome sends back answers, corrections, and the occasional polite observation that the question was wrong.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall expeditions keep arriving with maps of the Woods that MossHome did not draw, asking for corrections the archivists are not sure the forest wants made.
- A chair has come out wrong. Nine years of growing, and it leans. The grower insists the tree did it on purpose, and the archive has opened a file.

### Story Hooks

- The founding agreement's compost clauses include one measure that has never once been invoked. The elders know it by heart and do not say it aloud.
- The unreadable moss document has begun, for the first time in the records, to flower.
