## Lore

The accepted account is that the mountain is where the realm keeps its fire, and that it was burning before there was anyone to name it. It named the realm rather than the other way around, and everything else about its origin is told in stories that begin with "they say" and end a safe distance away.

### Recorded History

The mountain's history is kept by its neighbors: the dragonlings' eruption tallies, the gatherers' records of Gemfalls, and four centuries of watch ledgers observing, in essence, that it is still there. The mountain itself keeps no records - or keeps them somewhere nobody has managed to browse.

### Cultural Significance

The center of the realm by geography, gravity, and reputation. Its Gemfalls fund the foothills, its moods set the region's calendar, and its silhouette is the first thing every mapmaker draws and the last thing every traveler stops checking over their shoulder.

### Common Beliefs

- The mountain drops what it means to drop. Everything else, it is holding.
- Never thank the mountain out loud. It might take an interest.
- Red sky at night is the mountain minding its own business. Red sky at noon, mind yours faster.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the legend dragon is the mountain's tenant, warden, or prisoner. All three theories are held with confidence. The dragon has heard all three and commented on none.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The only living things permitted in the dragon's lair are the embermoths, who dust its scales. The moths have therefore seen more of the mountain's inside than every expedition in history combined, and, being moths, are telling no one.

### Neighbors

- **[Red Sky Foothills](/biomes/red_sky_foothills/)** — The mountain provides gemstones, warning tremors, and the weather; the foothills provide gathering law, the moth-watch, and an audience. It is the realm's longest-running relationship between unequals, and it works because the smaller party does all the thinking for both.
- **[The Expanse](/biomes/expanse/)** — The mountain closes the plateau's southern edge. Which of them is guarding which is a question the realm prefers not to need answered.

### Current Tensions

- Since the great eruption, the Gemfalls have not returned to their old rhythm, and the tally-keepers are running out of margin to disagree politely in.
- Expeditions keep aiming for the summit with Gemfall money and borrowed courage, and the foothill grandmothers have taken to interviewing them on the way up. Slowly. In shifts.

### Story Hooks

- Somewhere on the mountain is the entrance every treasure story agrees on and no returning expedition has ever confirmed. The stories are all secondhand. They are also all identical.
- The foothill bells have one ring that has never been used. Everyone knows what it would mean. Nobody has ever said it aloud.
