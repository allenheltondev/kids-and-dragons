## Lore

The accepted account is that the Exchange began as a seasonal market that failed to disperse. The stalls wintered over, the docks grew to meet the stalls, and by the time anyone thought to ask whose city it was, the honest answer was everyone's - which the city has kept as policy ever since.

### Recorded History

The city's history is commercial and proud of it: the first winter market, the building of the cliff docks, and a long tradition of absorbing every crisis in the realm as a change in prices. Seventeen founding plaques stand around the harbor, each sincere, each naming a different founder, all polished by the same civic fund.

### Cultural Significance

Where the realm meets itself. News, cargo, reunions, and campaigns all begin at the Exchange, and every people in the realm can find a meal here that tastes of home and a rumor that does not.

### Common Beliefs

- Anything lost anywhere in the realm surfaces at the Exchange within the year, priced fairly.
- Never ask a dock merchant where something came from. Ask where it is going - the answer is more honest and the story is better.
- The empty chair at an Exchange table is for whoever arrives next. Keeping it empty is not superstition; it is advertising.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Which people founded the city. Every people has a version placing themselves first, the seventeen plaques disagree in detail, and the dispute is considered part of the city's charm and is fanned accordingly.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The lost-and-found stalls' best supplier is a standing arrangement with the marsh boggarts. Neither side has ever put it in words, which both sides consider the strongest clause in it.

### Neighbors

- **[The Sunward Fields](/biomes/sunward_fields/)** — The Fields feed the city and the city pays for the Fields; each assumes the other would collapse first, and neither wants to check.
- **[Whispering Marsh](/biomes/whispering_marsh/)** — The marsh approach delivers remedies, guides, boggart salvage, and customers who prefer not to say which of those they came for.
- **[The Open Sea](/biomes/open_sea/)** — The docks are the city's fortune and its weathervane: whatever the sea is doing to shipping, the Exchange knows it by lunch and has priced it by dinner.

### Current Tensions

- The Gemfall rush made the Exchange its front door, and the city is now housing, feeding, and pricing a boom it publicly insists it planned for.
- The docks have outgrown the cliffs, and every expansion proposal involves somebody's ancestral mooring and everybody's opinion.

### Story Hooks

- An eighteenth founding plaque was commissioned, delivered, and never installed. It is in a warehouse. The name on it is a matter of intense civic non-discussion.
- The sea-returns shelf - things the ocean gave back decades late - includes one item the city's records list as delivered before it was lost. The clerk who filed it stands by the paperwork.
