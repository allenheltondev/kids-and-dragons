## Lore

Not established, and carefully so. The arch was already ancient when the marsh first had visitors to notice it, and every attempt to date it has concluded, in more careful words, "before us." The whispers pile up near the arch, which is how it was found and why it is avoided.

### Recorded History

The Gate's recorded history is a record of nothing happening, kept nervously: it has never opened, never moved, and never done anything but glow - and the glow keeping time with the red sky is the most thoroughly studied non-event in the realm.

### Cultural Significance

The realm's most respected closed door. Witches study it from a distance, frogfolk route the channels around it, and the phrase "said to be" does more work in sentences about the Gate than anywhere else in the realm.

### Common Beliefs

- It is a sealed portal to the world before the red sky. This is what everyone says, and what nobody can source.
- You do not knock. Whatever the Gate is, the one thing every people agrees on is that it is not to be knocked on.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Door, warning, monument, or lock - and if a lock, on which side. The theories are ancient, incompatible, and equally unencumbered by evidence.

### Neighbors

- **[Whispering Marsh](/biomes/whispering_marsh/)** — The marsh holds the Gate the way one holds a thing whose owner may return: channels routed around it, whispers thickest near it, and the frogfolk's markers giving it the widest, politest berth in the marsh.

### Current Tensions

- The runes have been brighter this year, and the whispers near the arch have changed pitch - facts nobody connects out loud, and everyone connects.
- Since the Gemfall, more than one visitor has noticed that the runes' hum and the scattered gemstones' hum are the same hum. The witches took notes. The notes are not circulating.

### Story Hooks

- The circle of still water around the arch does not flood, freeze, or drain, whatever the marsh does around it - a fact so old it reads as scenery, until you think about it.
