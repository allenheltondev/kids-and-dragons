The game has two views, and the mode only decides where they go.

- **The World view** is the shared picture: the scene painting, the heroes and whoever they
  are talking to, the narration, the dice, and the battle board.
- **Your view** is private to your phone: your hero, your choices, your bag.

## Party Mode: TV and phones

Open the game's TV address (`/tv/` and the four-letter code) in the TV's browser. The TV shows
the World view for everyone; each phone becomes that player's controller. This is the
living-room way to play.

## Travel Mode: phones only

Every phone shows one view at a time, and a shelf of painted objects along the bottom swaps
between them:

You can switch modes in the middle of a session. If the laptop driving the TV dies, a phone
can pick up the World view and the game carries on.

## The map

The map has two tabs.

- **World** is the painted map of the Realm of Red Sky, with the party's faces on the place
  the chapter is set. Zoom in a step at a time to look closer.
- **Route** draws tonight's chapter as a trail. The scenes you have already played are named;
  the ones ahead are still fog.
