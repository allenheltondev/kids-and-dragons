Every hero carries a bag with **six slots** and nothing to manage. Find something when the bag is
full, and the game asks: keep it and leave something behind, or leave it here.

There are no weapons or armor to juggle. What a hero wears comes from their class and tier.

## Things you use up

One use each. In a fight, using one is your action. Outside a fight, only the healing ones can be
used.

## Trinkets

Always working while they're in your bag.

{{< item-shelf kind="trinket" >}}

## Quest items

Keys, maps, letters and stones that matter to the story. They don't take a slot, and they're gone
when the campaign ends.

## Trading

At a Rest, open an item and pick a friend's name. Nothing moves until their phone says yes, and
the shared screen shows the offer so everyone knows what's happening.
