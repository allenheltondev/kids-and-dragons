A chapter is about thirty minutes of story, built from scenes. Every scene is one of five kinds.

<div class="wiki-rule-grid">
  <div class="wiki-rule-card"><h4>{{< icon "scroll" >}} Story</h4><p>The narration, then two to four choices. A turn marker passes around the party so everyone gets to choose.</p></div>
  <div class="wiki-rule-card"><h4>{{< icon "star" >}} Check</h4><p>Something uncertain. Someone rolls; see <a href="../dice/">Dice & Checks</a>.</p></div>
  <div class="wiki-rule-card"><h4>{{< icon "hand" >}} Choice</h4><p>A big decision. The whole party votes.</p></div>
  <div class="wiki-rule-card"><h4>{{< icon "lantern" >}} Rest</h4><p>Catch your breath. Spend new stat points, trade items, and look at each other's heroes.</p></div>
  <div class="wiki-rule-card"><h4>{{< icon "swords" >}} Encounter</h4><p>A fight on the battle board. See <a href="../combat/">Fights</a>.</p></div>
</div>

When a scene has people in it, they stand on the stage opposite the heroes: the merchant, the
grumpy toll troll, the frogfolk who will tell you the secret if you ask nicely. Every one of them
is painted; see the [Story Cast](../../cast/).

## Ending a chapter

A chapter ends in **success** or a **setback**. Both move the story on; a setback just pays half
the XP. Chapters also have bonus objectives, like "listened to the scholar everybody laughed at",
that pay the whole party or nobody. The end-of-chapter screen counts it all up.

In Gemfall, the party also shares a **purse** of coins that carries from chapter to chapter.

## Picking the adventure

If the household has more than one campaign going, each player votes in the lobby for which one
to play tonight. A tie goes to whoever holds the turn marker.
