There is exactly one kind of roll in Kids & Dragons:

> Roll a d20, add one of your stats, and try to reach the target number.

The phone tells you which stat and how hard it is, in words. The numbers behind the words are:

{{< difficulty >}}

A **natural 20** always works, no matter the target. A **natural 1** always misses.

The dice are rolled by the game, not by the phone, and the roll takes over the whole screen for a
moment: the d20 tumbles, any helper's die lands a beat later, and the sum ("14 + 3 + 2 = 19")
stays up long enough for everyone to read it. A success throws confetti; a natural 20 throws gold
confetti and stars. A miss kicks up a little dust, and a natural 1 topples over with a comic
*BONK!* and a ring of dizzy stars.

## Helping

While someone's check is waiting, any other player who is still standing can tap **Help** and add
their own die to the roll. The die gets bigger as the helper grows:

{{< help-dice >}}

One helper per check, you can't help your own roll, and each player can help once until the next
Rest or the next chapter. Choose your moment.

## Who rolls

Usually the party takes turns: whoever has rolled the fewest checks goes next, so nobody gets left
out. Some moments ask for the party's best roller in a stat. Others are a **party check**: everyone
rolls, and if half or more succeed, the party does.

## Missing is not losing

A failed roll does not stop the story. It sends it down a different path: the bridge breaks, the
merchant gets suspicious, the creature notices you. Some of the best scenes in the game are behind
a miss.
