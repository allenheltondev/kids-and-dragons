Making a hero takes four taps' worth of decisions: **species**, **class**, **stats**, and
**name**. A preview of your hero updates as you choose, and "Surprise me" will name them for
you.

## The four stats

Every roll in the game is one of these four. You spend **3 extra points** on top of the starting
values and your species' bonus.

{{< stat-cards >}}

Every hero also starts with **10 HP** and **4 steps** of movement each turn. **Guard**, the number
an enemy has to roll to hit you, is 11 plus your Quick.

## Species

Your species is what you are. Each one is born with a bonus, has one thing **only it can do**
in the story, and has one special move it can use **once in each fight**.

{{< species-roster >}}

The "only you can" abilities are not buttons. They show up as story choices when someone of the
right species is in the party: the bridge is out, so the dragonling can glide across; the guard
won't budge, so the kitsune can talk their way past. A party with different species sees
different doors.

## Classes

Your class is how you fight. Each has a signature move from the very first level, and learns a
new move at levels **3**, **6** and **9**.

{{< class-roster >}}

The class you pick also dresses your hero: the art above is each class's gear at Mythic tier,
the highest a hero can reach. See [Leveling Up](../leveling-up/) for how tiers work.
