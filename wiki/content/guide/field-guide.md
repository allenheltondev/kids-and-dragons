The Field Guide is a picture book of the Realm of Red Sky that the whole household shares. Every
page starts as a **?** card and fills in when the party first meets what's on it: starting a
chapter fills in the place, starting a fight fills in the creatures in it, and some scenes reveal
things along the way.

Each page remembers **who found it first**. New pages pop up as a note on the phone and a banner
on the TV ("New in your Field Guide"), and keep a **New** badge until somebody opens them.

The Field Guide is always one tap away on the shelf at the bottom of the phone, even in the middle
of a fight, and looking at it never changes anything in the game.
