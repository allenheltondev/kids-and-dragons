A fight happens on a battle board, a grid of 10 by 8 tiles painted for wherever the party is.
Moving diagonally costs one step, the same as moving straight.

## Turns

At the start of each fight everyone, heroes and enemies alike, rolls a d20 plus Quick, and that
is the order for this fight. On the TV, a banner announces each hero's turn.

On your turn, **move** up to your steps, then take **one action**. The phone only ever shows moves
you are allowed to make: tiles you can reach glow gold, and tapping one does it. No confirm button.

On top of these, you have your class's moves and your species' once-a-fight move. See
[Building a Hero](../heroes/).

## Knocked down, never dead

At 0 HP a hero lies down on the board and skips their turns until a friend helps them up. A
Songkeeper's Rally can do it from anywhere on the board. If every hero is knocked down, the fight
is lost and the story takes a different turn. Nobody is ever gone.
