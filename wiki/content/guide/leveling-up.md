XP is earned by the chapter, and **the whole party gets the same amount** for the story. On top
of that, every hero earns XP of her own for the rolls she makes that go well: a check she
succeeds, an attack that lands, a foe she knocks out, and a **natural 20** most of all. So heroes
level at their own pace.

Every level gives **one stat point** to spend at the next Rest. New class moves arrive at levels
3, 6 and 9 (see [Building a Hero](../heroes/)).

## Tiers

At levels 4, 7, 10, 15, 20 and 30, a hero grows into a new **tier**: Sworn, Radiant, Mythic,
Ascendant, Starborn and Legendary. The screen goes dark, the party gathers around, and the hero
transforms from one painting into the next, one hero at a time. Here is a dragonling up to Mythic:

{{< tier-strip species="dragonling" >}}

The three tiers past Mythic are new, and their paintings are still being made. Until they are, an
Ascendant, Starborn or Legendary hero is drawn as their Mythic self.

Past level 10 each level still gives a stat point and a little more health, and the most you can
put into one stat creeps up a notch at levels 15, 20, 25 and 30. The levels are far apart: a hero
needs 6,300 XP for level 10 and 80,300 for level 30.

A friend's tier also decides how big a die they add when they [help a roll](../dice/).

## Keeping what you earned

Anything gained during a campaign (levels, stat points, items) is *provisional* until the
campaign is finished. Finish it, and it is yours for good.

If a campaign has too many setbacks, it fails. The heroes go back to how they were when they
started it, but each one keeps a **souvenir**, a ribbon on their character sheet that says where
they've been. Anybody can open anybody's character sheet to see their level, what is still
provisional, and their souvenirs.
