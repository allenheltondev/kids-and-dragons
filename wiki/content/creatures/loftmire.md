## Lore

> *It is not chasing you. It is collecting.*

Nobody agrees what a loftmire is, which is why nobody agrees what to call one. The witches say loftmire and have a theory. The frogfolk say the low sky, because that is what fits on a chart. Boggarts say the shelf, and something taken by one has been shelved. The Exchange writes lifter on the paperwork and marks the cargo gone up. Everybody else, including every child in the marsh, says upfall. What is agreed is the sand. It comes out of the cloud and it is trying to get home, and a grain on its own manages it in a minute and nobody notices. A grain stuck to a sleeve is a very small thing pulling on a very large one, and losing. Enough grains stop losing. The cloud is never where the danger is; its dust is, and the cloud is only where the dust is going.

### For Fun

- Shedding, reclaiming, and shedding again, which appears to be the whole of it and has never been observed to stop.
- Standing over one bend of a channel for a whole afternoon, for no reason anybody has established, though everybody has a theory.
- Coming back. Whatever leaves with its sand on it will be followed, at walking pace, for as long as it takes.

### Getting Along

Two loftmires that meet drift through one another and come out the far side having exchanged some unknowable fraction of their sand, after which each spends a season retrieving grains that are arguably no longer theirs. The frogfolk consider this the single most tedious event in the marsh and reroute around it.

### Likes

- **[Boggart](/npcs/boggart/)** — Boggarts are the only people in the marsh who reliably give it back. They are also the sole reason it is now three miles from where it started.
- **[Will-o'-Wisp](/creatures/will_o_wisp/)** — A wisp that drifts inside becomes the lighting. It is also the only thing that ever hands a wisp a route it does not remember.

### Dislikes

- **[Frogfolk](/npcs/frogfolk/)** — They weight the poles, brush the coats, chart the drift, and give it nothing. A frogfolk channel is the emptiest crossing in the marsh.

## Getting Past It

There is nothing here to fight. These are the ways through.

- **Observation** (Clever, normal) — The reeds under it stand straight up and the water dimples upward. Read that, and read who in the party is already carrying how much.
- **Escape** (Quick, normal) — Get out from under it before the dose matters. It drifts at walking pace and it cannot turn.
- **Rescue** (Might, normal) — Someone going up can be held and brushed down. Two hands beat one, and the pull is gentle — it is only constant.
- **Environmental Problem Solving** (Clever, hard) — Carry a doseful and it follows. Walk it over the stuck barge, off the channel the children use, or away from the village — then put the sand down somewhere that does not matter, and go.
- **Restoring Habitat** (Heart, normal) — A stray followed something too far and cannot find the marsh. Getting it home is the same trick as luring it anywhere else, done kindly and for much longer.
