## Lore

> *The mountain's mood, printed on ten thousand small grey wings.*

Embermoths hatch in the warm cracks of the foothills and spend their whole lives migrating from one warmth to the next. Nobody taught them to predict eruptions; the moths simply have strong opinions about where it is comfortable, and the mountain publishes its plans as heat before it publishes them as smoke. Dragonlings learned to read them generations ago, and treat a settling swarm the way sailors treat a falling barometer.

### For Fun

- Basking in spirals on freshly warmed stone, in strict order of seniority. Nobody knows how the order is decided — not even, apparently, the moths.
- Riding cinder wolves between warm patches like slow ferries, which the wolves have agreed to never notice.
- Crowding a traveler's lantern in a polite ring, at exactly the distance where nobody's wings catch.

### Getting Along

An embermoth fears nothing that does not stamp. They land on children, tools, dragons, and soup, and regard all four as equally good perches. A swarm that leaves in a hurry is not being rude; it is being a warning.

### Likes

- **[Cinder Wolf](/creatures/cinder_wolf/)** — The warmest fur in the foothills, and it commutes.
- **[Dragonling](/characters/dragonling/)** — They keep warming-stones by their doors and have never once swatted.

### Dislikes

- **[Will-o'-Wisp](/creatures/will_o_wisp/)** — A light with no heat in it. The moths regard this as a broken promise.
