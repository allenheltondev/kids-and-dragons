## Lore

> *Keeper of the river's lost-and-found. The administration fee is usually nothing.*

Every silver otter keeps a hoard-shelf under a root somewhere: polished stones, dropped buttons, one perfect shell, arranged and rearranged over a lifetime. They return lost things to travelers not out of duty but because returning a thing makes you part of its story, and an otter would rather be in the story than own the button.

### For Fun

- Stone-polishing in the eddies under bridge pilings, where the current does most of the work and takes none of the credit.
- Elaborate mudslide runs, redesigned every season, graded harshly by the juveniles.
- Silent riverside trade meets with boggarts, where nothing is said and everything is appraised.

### Getting Along

Playful with anyone who is near water and not currently a hazard. An otter that surfaces holding something you lost last week is not magic; it is thoroughness.

### Likes

- **[Frogfolk](/npcs/frogfolk/)** — Pole boats are diving platforms, and a frogfolk pole has never once come down on an otter.
- **[Boggart](/npcs/boggart/)** — The only people who understand the true worth of a really good button.
- **[Stone Troll](/npcs/stone_troll/)** — It drops pebbles off the bridge on purpose and calls it maintenance.

### Dislikes

- **[River Drake](/creatures/river_drake/)** — It claims the deepest pool and the toll. An otter's stones should not count as cargo.
