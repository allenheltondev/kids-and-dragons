## Lore

> *A walking garden that takes the job as seriously as any gardener.*

A mosshorn's antlers are living wood. Moss, ferns, and whole seasons of small flowers grow there, and the oldest of the herd carry gardens that predate the towns near them. The forest seems to route its health through them: where a mosshorn grazes, things are well, and where a mosshorn will not walk, wise travelers also do not.

### For Fun

- Standing very still in the best light so the antler flowers open. The herd would deny this is showing off.
- Trading seedlings by gently knocking antlers together, the way other folk clink mugs.
- Wading into slow streams and letting minnows shelter in the antler roots until dusk.

### Getting Along

Shy but ceremonious. A mosshorn approaches a calm stranger exactly once, inspects them thoroughly, and then either stays forever or was never there. There is no appeal process.

### Dislikes
