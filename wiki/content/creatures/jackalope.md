## Lore

> *The fastest thing on the plains, and it needs you to know it.*

Nobody knows whether the antlers came first or the attitude. Jackalopes treat the realm's road network as a racetrack built specifically for them, and a caravan crossing the Plains is, whether it knows it or not, competing. The jackalope always wins, usually on a technicality only the jackalope understands.

### For Fun

- Racing caravans. The caravan is not always aware it has entered a race; this does not affect the standings.
- Antler-hooking contests over windfall apples, best of five, no biting.
- Assembling in perfect silence to watch travelers do absolutely anything at all.

### Getting Along

Bold at a distance, gone up close. A warren that lets you get near is telling you the neighbourhood is safe; an empty stretch of good road is telling you something else.

### Likes

- **[Centaur](/npcs/centaur/)** — They keep the roads flat and fast, and they pretend not to race back.
- **[Faun](/npcs/faun/)** — Orchard windfalls, and nobody ever counts them.

### Dislikes

- **[Cinder Wolf](/creatures/cinder_wolf/)** — Fair enough, being a wolf. But a wolf ruins a perfectly good straightaway.
- **[Kitsune](/characters/kitsune/)** — Raced one once. It turned out to be three of them, or possibly none. The warren has not recovered.
