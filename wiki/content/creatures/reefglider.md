## Lore

> *A kite the sea flies for itself.*

Reefgliders are the cove's weather, its festival, and its early-warning system in one slow-turning body of light. The patterns on their backs match the coral they grew up over, and their formation-signals are old enough that merfolk song borrows phrases from them rather than the other way around.

### For Fun

- Formation-flying through the reef arches in patterns that take a whole tide to complete.
- Orbiting merfolk song circles like a slow mobile, keeping time with their fins.
- Riding the front edge of a storm current the long way around the cove, apparently for the view.

### Getting Along

Reefgliders have no known dislikes, which the merfolk privately find suspicious. They investigate everything new by circling it at a courteous distance for exactly one afternoon, after which it is considered part of the reef.

### Likes

- **[Merfolk](/npcs/merfolk/)** — The song circles. The singers pretend not to be flattered by the audience, and fail.
- **[Selkie](/npcs/selkie/)** — Their wakes are the best game in the cove, and the selkies swim like they know it.
