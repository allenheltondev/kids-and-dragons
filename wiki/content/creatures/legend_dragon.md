## Lore

> *The mountain has a tenant. Nobody has read the lease.*

A legend dragon is less a species than an event that keeps happening to Mount Red Sky. Each one is singular, ancient, and organized around a purpose it has never seen the need to explain; whatever it keeps beneath the mountain, it has kept since before anyone now living could ask. Dragonling fireside stories claim kinship. The dragon has heard the stories. It has not commented.

### For Fun

- Rearranging the hoard by systems that take decades to complete. The current arrangement is believed to be by weight. The previous one may have been by regret.
- Listening to the mountain. It is possible this is a conversation.
- Testing visitors with questions it already knows the answers to, and grading generously when surprised.

### Getting Along

It may warn, test, bargain, or attack, and which one is a fact about the individual, not the species. What every account agrees on: it notices everything, forgets nothing, and finds almost all of it beneath comment.

### Likes

- **[Cloud Whale](/creatures/cloud_whale/)** — Something else old and slow that sings. The whales will not come near it, which it has decided not to notice.
- **[Embermoth](/creatures/embermoth/)** — The only living things permitted in the lair. They dust the scales, and they know the mountain's moods almost as well as it does.

### Dislikes

- **[The Deep Hollow Cult](/factions/deep_hollow_cult/)** — They keep knocking.
