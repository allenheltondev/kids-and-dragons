*A violent eruption from Mount Red Sky scatters powerful gemstones across the
realm, and the largest treasure rush in living memory begins. Three
adventurers — none of them chosen, all of them in a hurry — set out from
[The Exchange](/locations/exchange/) to reach the Gemfall before everyone
else. What they find is not treasure.*

## The Premise

The map has always said it plainly: Mount Red Sky, *source of the Gemfalls,
and restless power*. When the mountain erupts, gem-light rains across the
realm and every road, ship, and rumor converges on the neutral coastal city
of the Exchange. Merchants, scholars, mercenaries, pilgrims, and thieves pour
north. The party is three of them.

The stones are not volcanic treasure. They are fragments of an ancient seal
holding something dormant beneath the mountain — and every stone carried away
weakens it. Gemfall is a treasure hunt for five chapters and a race to undo a
treasure hunt for three.

## The Eight Chapters

### Act I — The Rush

1. **The City That Smelled Opportunity** — [The Exchange](/locations/exchange/).
   The eruption, the stampede, and the party: three adventurers, three
   motives. No fights in this chapter; the Exchange deals in people, prices,
   and promises.
2. **Green Roads North** — [The Sunward Fields](/biomes/sunward_fields/) to
   [Bramblewood](/locations/bramblewood/). The rush tramples the realm's
   food supply. Faun work-songs keep stopping mid-verse, harvest sprites
   reward the careful, and the hedge-keeper of Bramblewood will not open the
   thorns for a mob. Embermoths are drifting the *wrong way*.
3. **The Long Mile** — [The Plains](/biomes/plains/) to
   [Stone Crossing](/locations/stone_crossing/). Centaur guides, stolen road
   markers, and the crossing itself: troll tolls paid in riddles, a
   [river drake](/creatures/river_drake/) raising its rates, a rival
   expedition cutting the queue — and the current, which is not a metaphor.
   **A failed crossing does not stop the campaign.** The river simply sweeps
   the party west, into the marsh, by the wet door instead of the dry one.

### Act II — The Turn

4. **What the Marsh Remembers** — [Whispering Marsh](/biomes/whispering_marsh/)
   to [The Hollow Gate](/locations/hollow_gate/). New wisps by the dozen,
   frogfolk closing channels, a [mire mimic](/creatures/mire_mimic/) doing
   its finest work as an abandoned gem barge — and a boggart willing to
   trade the campaign's first real clue: the gemstones *hum*, and the Hollow
   Gate's runes hum back.
5. **The Forest Takes Attendance** — [Enchanted Woods](/biomes/enchanted_woods/)
   to [MossHome](/locations/mosshome/). The Woods have been quietly
   redirecting careless treasure hunters for weeks. Mosshorns will not walk
   near gem-carrying caravans, unicorns are tending wounded glades where
   fragments fell, and the Bigfoot path archives show the whole forest
   flinching away from the falls. The stones are older than the mountain.
6. **Ashfall** — [Red Sky Foothills](/biomes/red_sky_foothills/) to
   [Redspire Citadel](/locations/redspire_citadel/). Dragonling towns hold
   gathering law against a thousand strangers with picks, the
   [Deep Hollow Cult](/factions/deep_hollow_cult/) recruits the rush's
   ruined with kindness, and the [Skywardens'](/factions/skywardens/)
   watch-logs assemble the whole truth on one table: the gems are a seal,
   the seal holds a titan, and the rush is dismantling it by hand.

### Act III — The Race Back

7. **The Race Back Up the Mountain** — everywhere the party has been. The
   seal can be thickened if enough fragments come back, and nobody knows
   where the gems went better than the people who watched them go: a drake's
   hoard shelf, a boggart's catalogue, a mimic's swallowed satchel, an
   innkeeper's lost-and-found — and whatever the party sold in chapter one.
8. **Gemfall** — inside [Mount Red Sky](/locations/mount_red_sky/). The
   titan stirs. The [legend dragon](/creatures/legend_dragon/) — keeper of
   the seal since before the realm had names — asks the party questions it
   already knows the answers to. Then the choice: **restore** the seal,
   **destroy** what sleeps, **control** it, or **exploit** it. History files
   the party accordingly: the Heroes of Red Sky, or the treasure hunters who
   woke the mountain.

## Design Notes

- Failure reroutes; it never stops the campaign. The Stone Crossing branch is
  the signature example: the two outcomes change how chapter 4 *begins*, not
  whether the story continues.
- The Hollow Gate does not open in this arc. It foreshadows.
- The full design brief, with per-chapter flags, encounters, and canon
  scoping, lives in `docs/campaigns/gemfall.md`.
