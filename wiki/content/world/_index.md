The map says it plainly, right on the mountain: *"Source of the Gemfalls, and restless power."*
The **Realm of Red Sky** is a land where the forest remembers, the dead sleep uneasy at the river
mouth, and every road, ship, and rumor eventually ends up at the Exchange, a city that belongs to
no one.

In the north stands **Mount Red Sky**, the biggest mountain anybody knows, asleep and full of
gems. The **Great River** runs from the Glacier of Origins all the way down to the sea, splitting
the realm in two, and most of the stories in it begin with somebody needing to get across. The
**Sky Islands** drift in the northwest; the **Enchanted Woods** keep their promises in the
southwest; the **Eastern Plains** are where the caravans run.

Every pin below is a page. Gold pins are regions; violet pins are the places inside them.
