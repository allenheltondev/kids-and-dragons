## Lore

Road country. The accepted account is that the Plains have always been the space between other places, and are the only region proud of it: the roads came first, the camps grew where the roads rested, and the grass has been listening to travelers' news ever since.

### Recorded History

Plains history lives in the centaurs' route-songs, one verse per landmark with the hazards in the chorus. The songs record road openings, river shifts, and the start of the Shortcut Debate - a nine-generation argument that is by now a landmark itself, with its own verse.

### Cultural Significance

Neutral ground. Every people crosses the Plains, so the Plains belong to the crossing itself: news travels here faster than weather, and a campfire on the open grass is the realm's oldest meeting room.

### Common Beliefs

- All news arrives three days old, except bad weather, which is punctual.
- An empty stretch of good road is a message. Jackalopes are the punctuation.
- Never camp where the road bends. The road knows something.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the oldest roads were built or found. The centaurs say built, and sing the verses to prove it; the skeptics note the verses never say by whom.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The old east road does not end where the maps end it. Its bed runs on under the grass to the riverbank and stops at nothing, because the crossing it once met is gone - the river moved, and nobody now living knows there was ever a ford.

### Neighbors

- **[Western Branch of the Great River](/features/great_river_west_branch/)** — The branch cuts the southern Plains and decides, seasonally, which roads are roads and which are waiting.
- **[The Sunward Fields](/biomes/sunward_fields/)** — The Fields grow it and the Plains move it: the harvest road is the realm's supply line, and both regions maintain their half with the seriousness of people who have skipped a meal exactly once.
- **[The Eastern Plains](/biomes/eastern_plains/)** — Same grass, same sky, one river and a very firm distinction between them. Each side maintains that the other's roads are almost as good.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall traffic has doubled everything - caravans, campfires, opinions - and the route-songs are gaining verses faster than anyone can memorize them.
- Displaced cinder wolf packs keep crossing the roads at dusk, cold and far from home, and every campfire argues about whether to feed them.

### Story Hooks

- One route-song has a verse no landmark matches. The centaurs sing it anyway, in order, every time - a road is a promise, even to a place that is not there.
- The Shortcut Debate could be settled by walking the route. In nine generations, nobody has. It is generally understood that settling it would be a loss.
