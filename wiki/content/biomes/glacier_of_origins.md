## Lore

The name is the accepted account: the Great River begins here, and by yeti reckoning so does everything else worth remembering. The ice is the realm's oldest resident and its most complete record, laid down one winter at a time since before there were peoples to record.

### Recorded History

History here is not written but excavated: the yetis read events out of the ice, name meltwater years the way other peoples name children, and serve the important ones as tea. The reading rooms' catalogue runs back further than every archive in the realm combined, and the yetis will show you, slowly.

### Cultural Significance

The realm's source and its attic. Scholars, witches, and the occasionally desperate come north to consult memories the world has otherwise lost, and everything downstream - water, weather, history - starts on this ice.

### Common Beliefs

- Every drink from the river is somebody's remembered year. Drink respectfully.
- Do not shout on the glacier. Opinions differ on what you might wake, and the not-knowing is the rule.
- If the ice shows you something, it is yours to deal with now. Knowing makes you responsible.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the memories in the ice are records or residents. The yetis decline the distinction, which each side counts as support.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- At the bottom of one crevasse, below the oldest catalogued layer, the ice preserves a summer: green light, warm air, the smell of a season the north has not had in living memory. The yetis have not catalogued it, because filing it would mean deciding what it is.

### Neighbors

- **[Source of the Great River](/features/great_river_source/)** — The river rises from the melt, which means every region downstream is, strictly speaking, drinking the archive.
- **[Frostfang Peaks](/biomes/frostfang_peaks/)** — The peaks handle the fast half of the north - watch, rescue, wind - and the glacier handles the slow half - memory, melt, tea. Each considers the division generous to the other.

### Current Tensions

- The melt is changing: channels move seasons early, memories surface out of order, and downstream the river has begun delivering silt the Saltrot guides do not recognize.
- A frost wyrm has nested exactly where the next reading room was planned, and the yetis' response - wait a century or two - has, for the first time, struck some younger yetis as slow.

### Story Hooks

- A newly melted memory shows the river leaving by a different valley. The yetis have re-catalogued it three times, which is yeti for alarm.
- One meltwater year is never served as tea and never named. The vintage exists; the catalogue simply moves past it, and the politeness is glacial in every sense.
