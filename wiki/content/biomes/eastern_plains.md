## Lore

The accepted account is that the prides and the grass arrived together and have been taking care of each other since: the manticore homeland is not fenced or defended so much as walked, generation after generation, until the walking became the border.

### Recorded History

Pride history is carried in route-markers and told at the gathering grounds: the founding of the escort tradition, the establishment of the two crossings, and above all the riddle account with the trolls of Stone Crossing - a ledger of honor now old enough that its origin is itself a riddle nobody can answer.

### Cultural Significance

East of the river, safe passage is a manticore promise, and the realm has learned to read the silhouette on the ridge correctly: the scariest outline on the road is the reason the road is safe.

### Common Beliefs

- East of the river, wave first. Everything after that goes better.
- If you can see trouble coming for a day, you owe it a day of thinking.
- Never step over a pride marker; walk around. The detour is the point.

### Disputed Beliefs

- The riddle score. Both ends of the bridge keep the ledger, the totals have never once matched, and every proposal to audit them has been rejected by both sides as unromantic.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The famous formal greeting - performed at a distance, tail curled conspicuously away - is not ancient. It was composed by one mortified elder after an incident involving a fainting merchant, and the prides agreed within a generation to remember it as immemorial.

### Neighbors

- **[Stone Crossing](/locations/stone_crossing/)** — The southern door and the social calendar: escorts begin and end there, riddles are traded there, and a pride's reputation is half made in the bridge queue.
- **[Northern River Bridge](/routes/northern_river_bridge/)** — The northern door, quieter and colder, held in reserve; when Stone Crossing closes, the prides reroute the realm's traffic overnight and consider it good exercise.
- **[Frostfang Peaks](/biomes/frostfang_peaks/)** — The peaks watch the north and the prides watch the ground, and between them very little crosses the east unobserved.
- **[The Sallow Wastelands](/biomes/sallow_wastelands/)** — The southern flats are where pride escorts stop, hand travelers to the Saltrot guides, and wish everyone luck in a tone that means it.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall caravan volume has stretched the escort tradition thin, and some prides now book seasons in advance, which the elders regard as the end of civilization.
- The queue at Stone Crossing has grown long enough to cut across two prides' seasonal grounds, and the resulting etiquette problem is being negotiated at the pace etiquette deserves.

### Story Hooks

- One riddle has stood unanswered on the account for sixty years, accruing honor like interest. Solving it would make someone famous at both ends of the bridge; several careers have quietly been spent trying.
- An unclaimed pride marker stands far out on the eastern grass, older than any current pride and maintained by all of them, on the principle that somebody's promise should not lapse just because the somebody is gone.
