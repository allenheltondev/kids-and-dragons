## Lore

The accepted account is that the river made the Fields and the fauns merely negotiated: the floods left the finest soil in the realm, and the first cooperatives tamed the water one ditch at a time until the argument became an irrigation system.

### Recorded History

The Fields' history is a festival calendar with the history attached: every flood, drought, and triumphant beetroot is remembered as the festival founded about it. The cooperatives are older than money and keep their records in song, pie, and extremely durable grudges about weather.

### Cultural Significance

The realm eats because the Fields work. Every region knows it, the Fields never mention it, and the resulting politeness is one of the realm's great diplomatic assets.

### Common Beliefs

- Leave the corner rows. Everyone knows why, and nobody says why, and that is the arrangement.
- Never compliment the weather before the harvest is in. It gets ideas.
- A scarecrow that works too well should be introduced to everyone at the next festival, just in case.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Which village invented the honeycake. Four claim it, the recipes are identical, and the dispute is maintained with great care, because settling it would end four festivals.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The harvest sprites keep real ledgers, and the award ceremonies have minutes. If the big folk ever learned how much of the harvest is sprite arithmetic, the corner rows would get considerably bigger.

### Neighbors

- **[The Exchange](/locations/exchange/)** — The harvest road ends at the city's markets, and the city's appetite sets the Fields' spring planting. Each privately believes it is feeding the other.
- **[Stone Crossing](/locations/stone_crossing/)** — The eastern gate: everything bound over the river queues at the bridge, and a bad week at the crossing is a fine week for every wayside pie stand in the southern Fields.
- **[Bramblewood](/locations/bramblewood/)** — The far waystop, first to hear eastern news; the hedges decide who arrives, and the Fields have learned to trust the hedges' taste.
- **[Whispering Marsh](/biomes/whispering_marsh/)** — The Fields' ditches borrow the water and the marsh keeps the ledger; the two settle accounts through the frogfolk every dry year, and have never once been overdrawn.

### Current Tensions

- The Gemfall rush is hiring away farmhands at exactly the wrong time of year, and this harvest will be sung about either way.
- The Exchange keeps growing, and the planting maps have run out of polite places to expand.

### Story Hooks

- One festival on the calendar commemorates an event no record describes. It has the best food and the fewest explanations, and the elders' faces suggest the two facts are related.
- A flood generations back moved a boundary ditch, and two cooperatives have been happily farming each other's fields ever since. Surveys are periodically proposed and unanimously postponed.
