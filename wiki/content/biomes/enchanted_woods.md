## Lore

The commonly accepted account is that the Woods was here first and everything else was built around it, mostly with its permission. The forest has never confirmed this, but it has a way of routing paths past its favorite evidence.

### Recorded History

The bigfoot archives record the founding agreement of MossHome, centuries of path movements, and the night known as the Reshelving, when every trail in the forest moved at once and travelers woke up politely relocated. The archives classify it as maintenance.

### Cultural Significance

The realm swears its serious promises here, on the theory that the forest remembers them - which it does, with interest. It is also where people go to be lost on purpose, a service the Woods provides free of charge and with unnerving accuracy.

### Common Beliefs

- If you are lost, you were rude to something. Apologize in order of size.
- Never promise anything in the Woods you would not promise in writing.
- A path that is prettier than it was yesterday wants something.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the forest likes anyone, or merely keeps accounts. Unicorns insist it likes them. The bigfoot records note the question is unfalsifiable, and file fresh evidence on both sides annually.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- One clearing has never moved in the entire span of the archives. The forest is holding it for a promise that has not come due yet.

### Neighbors

- **[MossHome](/locations/mosshome/)** — The settlement is the forest's memory in writing and the forest is the settlement's memory in fact; neither could be itself without the other, and both know it.
- **[Whispering Marsh](/biomes/whispering_marsh/)** — The forest's waters drain south into the marsh, and marsh-folk say everything the Woods chooses to forget arrives downstream eventually, still muttering.
- **[Red Sky Foothills](/biomes/red_sky_foothills/)** — Ash from the mountain drifts onto the canopy in eruption years, and the forest reroutes paths away from the foothills for a season afterward, the way one avoids a neighbor after a loud party.

### Current Tensions

- Gemfall treasure hunters keep arriving with shovels, and the forest's opinion of shovels is being expressed one lost expedition at a time.
- A shrine path has not come back since spring, and the unicorns cannot agree on what the forest is feeling about it.

### Story Hooks

- The Reshelving has never been explained, and the archives' one marginal note about it - 'expected' - is in a hand nobody has identified.
- Somewhere in the Woods is a promise old enough that its keeping is now a geographic feature.
