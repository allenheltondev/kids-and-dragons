## Lore

Every island keeps its own founding story, and at least four of them open with "the first island, alone in the sky." The version told to children everywhere is simpler: the islands got tired of the sea and left, and the wind, flattered by the compliment, has carried them ever since.

### Recorded History

Island history is sung, not shelved - a settlement's past is its navigation songs, with the disasters in the chorus. The one date every island agrees on is the Great Becalming, a windless season when the archipelago drifted into one enormous neighborly pile-up and the messenger tradition was founded by people who could suddenly walk to their relatives.

### Cultural Significance

To the surface realm, the islands are where the weather is decided and where letters go to take the long way. To the kitsune they are home in the only sense that matters for a moving address: not a place you are, but a place you can find again.

### Common Beliefs

- An island that falls from the sky becomes an ordinary island in the sea, which is why no navigator fully trusts a suspiciously round island.
- Whistling on a windless day is asking the wind a question, and the wind answers all questions personally.

### Disputed Beliefs

- Whether the islands follow the cloud whales or the cloud whales follow the islands. Every temple holds a firm position, and the whales decline to settle it.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The foundation stones of the oldest temple are fossilized barnacle beds. Whatever the founding songs say, the islands have been sea floor, and the sea may not consider the loan closed.

### Neighbors

- **[The Open Sea](/biomes/open_sea/)** — Everything dropped from an island eventually arrives in the sea, which the kitsune treat as a postal service with no return address and the sea treats as tribute.
- **[Frostfang Peaks](/biomes/frostfang_peaks/)** — The high wind routes pass within sight of the griffin watch, and the two peoples trade weather news by signal - the realm's oldest conversation conducted entirely in flags.
- **[The Exchange](/locations/exchange/)** — The city's docks are where island goods touch the ground, and kitsune messengers treat the place as the realm's front desk.

### Current Tensions

- The recurring wind routes of two islands have begun to overlap, and both communities are being extremely polite about it.
- A messenger route that ran for three generations lost its wind this spring and has not found it again.

### Story Hooks

- A letter posted during the Great Becalming is still in circulation, redirected each season because its address keeps moving.
- An island last sighted a generation ago is, by the oldest songs' arithmetic, due back.
