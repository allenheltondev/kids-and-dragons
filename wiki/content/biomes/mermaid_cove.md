## Lore

The accepted account is that the reef built the cove, the cove sheltered the peoples, and the alliance made it home: merfolk below, selkies between, and an agreement with the land old enough that its signing predates every surviving record of what, precisely, it was signed against.

### Recorded History

Cove history is the treaty and its amendments - most of them clarifications of what counts as a promise, filed after some traveler made one casually - plus the unbroken run of nightly song circles, which the merfolk date the way other peoples date dynasties.

### Cultural Significance

The realm's southern door to the sea, and its standing proof that an agreement can outlive its reasons and still hold. Sailors reckon the cove the kindest water on the map, and are right, and are rarely told why the singing is not optional.

### Common Beliefs

- A rescued swimmer owes the cove a story, payable at dinner. The dinner is not optional either.
- Never sing near the Whirlpool unless you mean it. It listens more closely than anything else in the sea.
- Casual promises do not exist in the cove. There are only promises.

### Disputed Beliefs

- What the alliance was made against. Every people has a theory, the elders answer every version with the same courteous nothing, and the treaty's first page is a list of signatories under a title nobody can read anymore.

### Hidden Truths

> *Creator canon — not generally known in-world. No NPC recites these.*

- The oldest verses of the nightly song are not merfolk compositions. They are borrowed reefglider signal patterns, older than the alliance, and the gliders still answer them - which the singers have decided is harmony rather than correspondence.

### Neighbors

- **[The Whirlpool](/locations/the_whirlpool/)** — The cove's standing appointment: the songs keep it from widening, the Whirlpool keeps turning, and neither party has ever missed a night.
- **[The Bone Yard](/locations/bone_yard/)** — On still nights the song circles sing a little louder for the neighbors across the water, and the Yard is measurably quieter for it. Neither shore has ever acknowledged the arrangement.
- **[The Open Sea](/biomes/open_sea/)** — Everything arrives from it and everything is eventually owed back to it; the cove is the sea's guest room, kept beautiful on purpose.
- **[The Exchange](/locations/exchange/)** — Sea trade and selkie couriers bind the cove to the city's docks closely enough that cove news reaches the Exchange while it is still gossip.

### Current Tensions

- The song circle rosters are thinning as young selkies and merfolk take courier and dock work at the Exchange, and the elders have begun, gently, to count attendance.
- Gemfall shipping lanes have edged closer to the Whirlpool to save a day's sailing, and the cove is drafting an amendment about it.

### Story Hooks

- One treaty amendment is sealed and unread, to be opened 'when it is needed.' Nobody knows how they will know. It is checked, unopened, every year.
- The year the songs went unsung is remembered by the attendance customs it created and by nothing else; what happened that year was either nothing, or the reason for everything since.
