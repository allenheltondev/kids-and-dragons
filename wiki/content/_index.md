**Kids & Dragons** is a tabletop-style adventure for a family of three: two grown-ups and a
kid, played on the TV with phones as controllers, or on phones alone in the back of the car.
You build a hero, a unicorn or a dragonling or a bigfoot who is very good at hugs, and play
the story a chapter at a time.

The story is set in the **Realm of Red Sky**, a land of drifting sky islands, a forest that
remembers, and a sleeping mountain with gems in its heart. This wiki covers both: how the game
plays, and everything that lives in the world.
