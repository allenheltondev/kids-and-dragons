## Lore

> *The only people who can walk on the Saltrot, and the only ones who would want to.*

Skuppers move in quick little skips across mud that swallows boots, and they live in burrow towns dug into the flats with air kept in pockets below. Every great flood resets the town, so nothing they build is meant to last and nobody minds. Puffing up is not a choice: a startled skupper goes round and floats, which on the flats is useful. A puffed guide bobbing in a flooded channel is a buoy for anyone stuck, and the polite thing is to hold on and not mention it afterward.

### For Fun

- Crab clocks. Fiddler crabs wave at the turning tide, and a good skupper can tell the time to the minute by how many are waving.
- Crest signaling. Greetings, warnings and gossip travel across the flats faster than anyone can walk them.
- Flood naming, argued over for a whole season. A flood is not properly over until it has a name everyone can live with.

### Getting Along

Friendly, nosy and frank. A skupper guide will tell you your boots are wrong, your route is wrong and your lunch smells interesting, all before you have finished saying hello. Some guides are generous, some strict, a few famously grumpy, and every one of them refuses to take anybody to the buried bridge.

### Likes

- **[Frogfolk](/npcs/frogfolk/)**: The neighbors at the fresher edge of the flats. Frogfolk boats bring the river's news down, and skupper guides take it the rest of the way.

### Dislikes

- **[Bone Crawler](/creatures/bone_crawler/)**: They swarm anything with fresh magic in it, and the flats are the only way to the Bone Yard, so it is the guides who keep pulling travelers out of the swarm.
