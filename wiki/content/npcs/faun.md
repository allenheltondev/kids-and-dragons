## Lore

> *The harvest is a song, and everyone is in the chorus whether they know it or not.*

Fauns farm the Sunward Fields in cooperatives older than money, and their music is not decoration — the beat is the schedule, the harmony is the division of labor, and a field's work-song tells you what stage the season is in from half a mile away. A faun who stops singing mid-verse has seen something worth stopping for, and every hand in earshot knows it.

### For Fun

- Festival-inventing. First frost, last frost, a notably good pumpkin: the calendar has more festivals than days, which the fauns consider a scheduling triumph.
- Building instruments out of farm surplus — gourd-drums, seed-shakers, and the irrigation flute, which waters a row while you play it.
- Competitive pie diplomacy. Recipes are state secrets. Judges are bribed openly, in pie.

### Getting Along

Warm, rhythmic, and incapable of letting a stranger stand at the edge of anything. Every exchange becomes an invitation and every answer an anecdote; being adopted by a faun village takes roughly one meal.

### Likes

- **[Harvest Sprite](/npcs/harvest_sprite/)** — The fields' other workers. A faun leaves the corner rows unpicked, and both parties call it good manners.
- **[Centaur](/npcs/centaur/)** — The harvest goes somewhere and the news comes back. Also they can carry a drum you can climb inside.
- **[Jackalope](/creatures/jackalope/)** — The orchard's least useful employees, kept on for morale.

### Dislikes

- **[Boggart](/npcs/boggart/)** — Every festival, exactly one pie. Always exactly one. They bake an extra now and set it slightly apart, and everyone involved maintains the fiction.
