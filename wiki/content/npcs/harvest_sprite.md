## Lore

> *The realm's smallest professionals, and by a wide margin its most serious.*

Harvest sprites tend everything the big folk overlook: the crooked row, the dropped seed, the plant behind the barn that nobody planted. No sprite has ever explained the arrangement, but communities that leave a little food out for small creatures find their fields doing better than arithmetic says they should, and no sprite will confirm the connection while sober enough to be quoted.

### For Fun

- Competitive tidying. Two sprites, one messy row: it is a race now, and it was always a race.
- Riding jackalopes. Without the jackalope's knowledge. Briefly.
- Award ceremonies for plants. The season's best beetroot receives a small woven medal. Attendance is mandatory.

### Getting Along

Earnest, over-explaining, and heartbroken by refusal for about four seconds at a time. A sprite offers help nobody asked for, takes enormous pride in work nobody sees, and remembers every person who ever said thank you to something smaller than themselves.

### Likes

- **[Faun](/npcs/faun/)** — The only big folk whose work-songs have a verse for the small workers.
- **[Mosshorn](/creatures/mosshorn/)** — An antler posting is the profession's highest honor: a garden that walks, with tenure.

### Dislikes

- **[Jackalope](/creatures/jackalope/)** — It eats the overlooked plants. The overlooked plants are the entire jurisdiction.
- **[Boggart](/npcs/boggart/)** — Recovered items keep un-recovering. The paperwork is endless, and the paperwork is imaginary, and it is still endless.
