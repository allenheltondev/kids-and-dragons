## Lore

> *Nobody in the flock has ever had the first idea. The flock has had all of them.*

Sheeple graze the low ridges of the Plains in flocks of anywhere from three to three hundred, and nothing a sheeple does was its own idea. One starts to walk, and the rest walk. One sits down to watch the sunset, and the hillside sits down. Ask a sheeple who decided and it will point, sincerely, at the sheeple next to it, who will point at the next. The flock still gets somewhere. Things get decided, songs get sung, and outfits get invented. Where the first idea comes from is the oldest question on the Plains, and the sheeple are the least bothered by it. When a flock comes apart it splits into threes, never ones or twos, and each trio lifts its head and lets out the Gather: a single enormous call that carries across three valleys and means one thing only, come here. Every sheeple who hears it comes.

### For Fun

- Follow-the-follower: a long, slow ring of sheeple walking around a hilltop, each following the one in front. It has gone on since spring. Nobody started it.
- Shearing Day. The whole flock shears on the same morning, because one of them did, and by evening it has felted a matching new wardrobe of hats, rings, and tassels that looks like nothing anyone has worn before.
- Copying the caravans. A flock that once watched centaurs read each other's sashes spent a month solemnly studying each other's scarves.
- Leaning with the wind. When the grass bends, the flock bends, and it calls that dancing.

### Getting Along

Friendly, warm, and skittish. Meeting a flock starts with a full minute of silence while every sheeple waits for another to say hello, and then twenty hellos at once. They get close quickly and bolt even faster. A shout, a snapping branch, or a jackalope in the grass sends a flock scattering in threes. Then come the Gather calls, the regathering, and a sheepish return that pretends nothing happened. The way to befriend them is to be still, gentle, and patient. Win over one trio and the rest of the flock decides it likes you too, and starts doing whatever you do.
