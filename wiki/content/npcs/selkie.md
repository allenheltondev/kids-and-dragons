## Lore

> *Two shapes, one joke, always delivered on time.*

Selkies are the realm's couriers between two worlds that would otherwise only wave at each other. The coat is not a garment but a self, and everything in selkie culture flows from that one fact: where it hangs, who may touch it (no one), and the exact height of shelf that means I trust this house. A selkie tells the truth in a way that sounds like a joke, which keeps the truth portable.

### For Fun

- Trading jokes up and down the coast. A joke's worth is measured in how far it traveled to reach you, and a really good one arrives salt-stained.
- Message-racing the tide: the letter must beat the water to the third rock. The letter usually wins. The occasions it did not are famous.
- Storm-swimming for fun, an activity every other people on the coast has independently classified as a rescue situation.

### Getting Along

Easy, quick, and register-shifting — a selkie talks to a merfolk elder, a dockhand, and a child in three different voices inside one minute, and means every word in all three. Their friendship is loyal and their coat is not a topic.

### Likes

- **[Merfolk](/npcs/merfolk/)** — Kin by treaty and temperament, the temperament being reliability. With jokes.
- **[Silver Otter](/creatures/silver_otter/)** — Kindred spirits: swimmers who understand that an errand is improved by a detour.

### Dislikes

- **[Boggart](/npcs/boggart/)** — No boggart has ever taken a coat. Every selkie hangs it higher near one anyway, and both peoples know it is not fair, and the shelf stays where it is.
