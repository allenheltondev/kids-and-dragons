## Lore

> *Ask one how far. You will get the distance, the weather, and one warning, once.*

Centaurs have walked the roads since before the roads were roads, and the old routes are taught to foals as songs — one verse per landmark, with the hazards in the chorus so nobody forgets them. A centaur's caravan sash is also a map, embroidered a stitch at a time over a working life, and reading a stranger's sash is how centaurs shake hands.

### For Fun

- Sash embroidery. A route walked is a route stitched, and an elder's sash is a life you can read.
- Relay runs for the joy of it: how fast can news cross the realm? The record is disputed, loudly, at every campfire.
- The Shortcut Debate — a nine-generation argument about one hypothetical route through the foothills. No centaur alive believes it exists. No centaur alive will concede.

### Getting Along

Unhurried, factual, and impossible to alarm. A centaur states the distance, the weather, and the trouble in the same level tone, and never repeats a warning — the road is honest, and expects the same of you.
