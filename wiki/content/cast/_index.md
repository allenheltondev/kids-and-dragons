When a scene has someone in it, they stand on the story stage opposite the party: a toll troll
with a ledger, a fortune-teller with three cards, a grandmother dragonling who has seen all of
this before. Each one below is the figure the game draws, sorted by the chapter where the party
first meets them. Tap a portrait to see it full size.
